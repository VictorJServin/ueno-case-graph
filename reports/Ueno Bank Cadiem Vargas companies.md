# Ueno lent fortunes against negligible income

Cadiem is a victim of the Ueno Bank collapse on the public record, not a party to Case 89/2026: it is Paraguay's largest mutual-fund manager, its funds held paper issued by Ueno Bank and two unnamed group companies, the securities regulator froze five of its funds for three days, and clients then asked to withdraw about 40% of the roughly US$400 million it manages. No prosecutor, regulator or outlet found names Cadiem or its officers as accused, raided or sanctioned, and no ownership link to Grupo Vázquez is reported. Mauro Vargas Wildberger is one of the 27 people charged on 4 November 2026; the press knows him only as president and reported 99.62% shareholder of the hotel company Franede S.A. and 4.35% shareholder of the insurance broker AIME S.A., he is at liberty with a measures hearing set for 18 December 2026, and no statement from him or a lawyer was found. On the companies, the central finding is an asymmetry: audited figures exist for only four entities (Grupo Vázquez, ITTI, Terravaz and the bank itself), and for the borrowers the only two published income figures are tiny against their credit. Grupo Hendy reportedly averaged G. 73 million a month in 2025 against about G. 252,521 million of bank debt, and Grupo M (Pagopar) showed period income of G. 10,576 million against US$62.0 million of credit. For Mowi, TUIU, Franede, AIME, SATEC, HD Invest, La Tropa Fletes, Factopar and most of the second ring, no income figure has been published at all. Every statement about misuse of funds below is an allegation by the Central Bank (BCP), the prosecution or the press quoting them; no court has ruled, and everyone named is presumed innocent.

A note on figures: amounts are written in English number format. "G. 1,000 million" means one thousand million guaraníes. Dollar equivalents appear only where the cited outlet printed them. Rating-report figures were machine-extracted from PDFs and should be re-checked against the originals before reuse. Research cut-off is 20 November 2026.

## Cadiem held Ueno paper and faced a US$160 million run

### A 23-year-old broker with 18.6% of the fund industry

Cadiem operates as two linked firms: CADIEM Casa de Bolsa S.A., a brokerage, and Cadiem Administradora de Fondos (AFPISA), a fund administrator. It also runs a Global Markets division for foreign investments, places corporate bonds and advises multilaterals. At the end of the first half of 2026 it reported **US$1,458 million of client assets** (US$872 million in brokerage, US$440.6 million in mutual and investment funds, US$146.1 million in international business), up 26% from US$1,154 million at end-2025, and **18.6% of industry fund assets**, described as the largest share, across ten funds ([Revista PLUS, 30 Jul 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/07/30/cadiem-crecio-26-en-primer-semestre-y-consolido-su-liderazgo-en-atraccion-de-emisores/)). November coverage gives the fund arm alone as about US$400 million with 23 years in the market ([Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/cadiem-enfrenta-pedidos-de-rescate-por-us-160-millones-tras-la-crisis-de-ueno/)), about 20,000 clients ([ABC Color, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/11/ueno-bank-que-piden-los-inversores-de-casa-de-bolsa-afectados-y-cuando-cobraran-los-retiros/)) or more than 22,000 clients and over 130 staff with offices in Asunción, Itapúa and Alto Paraná ([La Nación, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/17/grupo-financiero-argentino-acuerda-compra-de-acciones-de-cadiem/)). The two client counts are approximations from different outlets. Elías Gelay is reported as president of the brokerage and César Paredes as president of the fund administrator ([Revista PLUS, 30 Jul 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/07/30/cadiem-crecio-26-en-primer-semestre-y-consolido-su-liderazgo-en-atraccion-de-emisores/); [5Días, 10 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/article/cadiem-cuestiona-a-la-superintendencia-y-alerta-sobre-la-prdida-de-confianza)). The Superintendencia de Valores (SIV) of the BCP supervises the funds ([Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)).

Cadiem's shareholders are not named in any article found, and no rating of Cadiem or its funds was retrieved. Ownership is about to change regardless: on 17 November 2026 the Argentine group Balanz Capital announced a memorandum of understanding to acquire the majority of the brokerage and the fund administrator, subject to about 30 days of due diligence, final documents and regulatory approval, with neither price nor percentage disclosed. La Nación says the deal ran into difficulties after Ueno's collapse ([La Nación, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/17/grupo-financiero-argentino-acuerda-compra-de-acciones-de-cadiem/)).

### The exposure: instruments held, valued at zero by order of the regulator

Cadiem told investors that some of its funds hold instruments issued by Ueno Bank and by two companies of its economic group, which it did not name, and that exposure varies by fund; it gave no amounts and stressed that fund units are not deposits at the bank ([Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)). The only percentage in print is **11.73% of one unnamed fund's equity** linked to the Ueno economic group as of 3 November 2026 ([Última Hora, c. 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/panico-financiero-bonos-soberanos-caen-y-casa-de-bolsa-sufre-demanda-del-40)). That figure belongs to a single fund and cannot be applied to the whole US$400 million. Which two group companies are involved is unreported; FIX SCR counted about US$181 million of group capital-market bonds outstanding at December 2025 (US$124 million Grupo Vázquez, US$41 million ITTI, the rest Terravaz) ([FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)), which makes those issuers plausible candidates, but no source says so.

The sequence ran as follows. On 3 November Cadiem said it was following the BCP's resolution procedure and assessing effects ([ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-mensaje-de-cadiem-a-sus-inversionistas-tras-el-anuncio-del-bcp/)). Under SIV Resolution SV.SG. N.º 0015/2026, redemptions and subscriptions were suspended in five funds: Disponible Renta Fija en Guaraníes, Disponible Dólares Americanos, Proyección Guaraníes, Proyección Dólares and Crecimiento Renta Fija en Guaraníes. The SIV suspended trading in Ueno Bank instruments and ordered them **valued at zero** during the resolution, with criteria for the related companies still under review; Cadiem said it could not yet say how much would be recovered ([Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)). On 6 November the SIV lifted the suspension for Cadiem and Puente; BASA and BNB Asset Management funds had already resumed, and only Ueno AFPISA's two funds stayed frozen ([Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/bcp-levanta-restricciones-a-fondos-de-cadiem-y-puente-pero-mantiene-las-de-ueno/); [Diario HOY, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/06/ueno-sigue-sin-habilitar-retiros-de-fondos-mutuos-bcp-explica-que-pasa-con-el-dinero-de-los-inversores)).

Reopening triggered the run. Redemption requests reached about **40% of assets, roughly US$160 million**, and Cadiem said meeting them at once would force sales into a volatile market ([Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/cadiem-enfrenta-pedidos-de-rescate-por-us-160-millones-tras-la-crisis-de-ueno/); [La Nación, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/cadiem-arma-un-plan-de-contingencia-tras-solicitudes-de-rescate-por-el-40-de-sus-fondos/)). It paid in stages with SIV approval: earlier requests between 6 and 16 November, requests of 9 to 13 November between 17 and 23 November, later ones with no confirmed date, at the 5 November unit value. Cadiem said it had no compensation proposal, that the BCP provides no money and that the deposit guarantee does not cover mutual funds. An investor group (more than 500 members per ABC, 345 per 5Días) asked for per-fund exposure, the investment limits applied, a waiver of early-withdrawal charges and for Cadiem to absorb part of the loss with its own funds; it also disputed a stated 5% payout limit that Cadiem's own worked example put near 15% ([ABC Color, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/11/ueno-bank-que-piden-los-inversores-de-casa-de-bolsa-afectados-y-cuando-cobraran-los-retiros/); [5Días, 10 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/article/cadiem-cuestiona-a-la-superintendencia-y-alerta-sobre-la-prdida-de-confianza)). Cadiem promised consolidated data for 12 November; that data was not found.

### No role in the alleged scheme has been reported

Cadiem does not appear among the 27 accused or the companies named in the case ([Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/quienes-son-y-que-rol-tuvieron-los-27-imputados-en-el-caso-ueno-bank)). The regulatory action that touched it was prudential and applied to every manager with Ueno-linked holdings ([Diario HOY, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/06/ueno-sigue-sin-habilitar-retiros-de-fondos-mutuos-bcp-explica-que-pasa-con-el-dinero-de-los-inversores)). No raid, complaint, sumario or sanction involving Cadiem was found, and no article reports a corporate or family tie to Grupo Vázquez. Neither the FIX report on Grupo Vázquez nor the stock exchange's statement on the crisis names Cadiem as arranger of group bonds ([FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf); [Revista PLUS, 11 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/11/bolsa-de-valores-aclara-su-rol-en-la-crisis-de-ueno-y-deslinda-responsabilidades/)). Two indirect business contacts are on record. IDB Invest funded its 2024 guaraní loan to Ueno (G. 150,000 million, about US$20 million) through bonds issued on the Paraguayan market with CADIEM Casa de Bolsa as broker ([IDB Invest, 18 Nov 2024](https://www.idbinvest.org/en/news-media/idb-invest-and-ueno-bank-join-forces-promote-financial-inclusion-paraguay)), and Cadiem placed G. 360,000 million of BID Invest bonds in the first half of 2026, though no source says what those later proceeds financed ([Revista PLUS, 30 Jul 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/07/30/cadiem-crecio-26-en-primer-semestre-y-consolido-su-liderazgo-en-atraccion-de-emisores/)). Brokering a multilateral's bonds is not participation in the lender's borrower.

Cadiem's problem is of a different kind from the intermediaries that failed. The stock exchange suspended Ueno Casa de Bolsa and Valores Casa de Bolsa on 5 November for failing to fund repo settlements ([ABC Color, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/06/ueno-bank-bva-suspende-a-ueno-y-valores-casa-de-bolsa-por-falta-de-fondos-para-cumplir-operaciones/)), and about 30,000 investors in Ueno-linked mutual funds were still waiting on 15 November ([Diario HOY, 15 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/15/unos-30000-inversionistas-de-fondos-mutuos-en-ueno-aguardan-una-solucion)). Cadiem held paper that lost value and then met a liquidity squeeze; it did not default. The open question is whether its funds respected their concentration limits, which is exactly what investors have asked it to document. All of this rests on press reports; Cadiem's own sites, fund fact sheets and the SIV resolutions were not read directly.

## Mauro Vargas Wildberger appears in public only as a shareholder of two borrowers

Nothing was found about his career before the case: no interviews, appointments, trade-press items or company announcements. Every public description comes from the charging coverage. He is listed as number 23 of the 27, "accionista presidente de Franede SA y accionista de Aime SA Corredores de Seguros" ([Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/quienes-son-y-que-rol-tuvieron-los-27-imputados-en-el-caso-ueno-bank)). Outlets give his Franede holding as **99.62%**, as shareholder and beneficial owner, and his AIME holding as **4.35%** ([Hoy, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/10/imposicion-de-medidas-para-19-imputados-por-el-vaciamiento-de-ueno-bank); [Amambay News, 4 Nov 2026](https://amambaynews.com/ueno-bank-imputacion-expone-como-intentaron-ocultar-quiebre-a-traves-de-venta-simulada.html)). RDN calls him "el empresario" and president of Franede ([RDN, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.rdn.com.py/2026/11/13/allanan-estancia-del-grupo-vazquez-por-esquema-de-fraude)). One sentence in ABC Color says the BCP identifies someone as "funcionario del grupo y familiar de Miguel Vázquez" in the passage about Franede's shares passing to him; the retrieved text does not make the referent explicit, so his employment inside any Grupo Vázquez company remains unconfirmed, with no job title, employer or dates in any source ([ABC Color, 19 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/19/costosos-inmuebles-parte-del-esquema-de-triangulacion-de-ueno/)). No other company, public tender or state contract naming him surfaced; La Política Online's remark that he and AIME's president are cross-linked through "several associated firms" names none and probably refers to Franede and AIME themselves ([La Política Online, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/justicia-py/ya-son-27-los-imputados-por-el-colapso-de-ueno-y-vazquez-es-el-unico-con-prision-preventiva/)).

The business facts that matter concern timing. ABC Color, citing the BCP report, says Franede belonged to Grupo Vázquez until **30 June 2025**, when its shares passed to him ([ABC Color, 19 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/19/costosos-inmuebles-parte-del-esquema-de-triangulacion-de-ueno/)). The prosecution's reported position is that Franede and several other borrowers were owned by ITTI or Grupo Vázquez immediately before receiving credit, and that the bank lent to him as Franede's president before he "ended up buying" the group's shares ([Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/quienes-son-y-que-rol-tuvieron-los-27-imputados-en-el-caso-ueno-bank); [Judiciales.net, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.judiciales.net/peligro-de-fuga-y-expectativa-de-pena-fundan-pedido-de-prision-para-vazquez/)). The first reported Franede loan, US$24.9 million over ten years, is dated 28 August 2025, eight weeks after the transfer ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)). No outlet reports the price or terms of the share transfer, and no registry entry for it was located. Three outlets also report the prosecution's claim that he is related by affinity to the principal defendant, which prosecutors use to argue common control of the borrowers and the bank's related-party limits; that claim is included here only for its role in the case ([Judiciales.net, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.judiciales.net/peligro-de-fuga-y-expectativa-de-pena-fundan-pedido-de-prision-para-vazquez/); [RDN, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.rdn.com.py/2026/11/13/allanan-estancia-del-grupo-vazquez-por-esquema-de-fraude)).

His procedural position is the lightest tier in the case. He was charged on 4 November 2026 by prosecutors Jorge Arce, Silvio Corbeta, Yeimy Adle and Francisco Cabrera, in the file captioned Expediente 89/2026 "Federico Miguel Vázquez Villasanti y otros s/ Lesión de Confianza y otros" ([Última Hora, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/fiscalia-imputa-a-los-8-directivos-de-ueno-bank-y-a-otras-19-personas-de-empresas-asociadas); [La Nación, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/04/caso-ueno-bank-imputan-a-27-personas-y-piden-prision-de-miguel-vazquez/)). The counts attributed to the 27 as a group are breach of trust, improper conduct in crisis situations, attempted fraud and breach of the duty to keep commercial books, for conduct in 2025 and 2026; no outlet breaks them down per person ([ABC Color, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/04/caso-ueno-imputan-a-exdirectivos-por-supuesta-lesion-de-confianza-y-otros-delitos/); [La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/)). He is not detained. Prosecutors sought only a travel ban and personal or real bail for the 19 company-linked defendants, and his hearing on measures is set for **18 December 2026** alongside two TUIU defendants; a general ban on selling or encumbering assets already applies to all of them ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/caso-ueno-citan-a-directivos-coprocesados-en-diciembre-para-la-imposicion-de-medidas); [Hoy, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/10/imposicion-de-medidas-para-19-imputados-por-el-vaciamiento-de-ueno-bank)). Prosecutors also asked for account freezes through the BCP; whether that was granted for him is not confirmed ([ABC Color, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/04/caso-ueno-imputan-a-exdirectivos-por-supuesta-lesion-de-confianza-y-otros-delitos/)). None of the articles read carries a statement from him or a lawyer, and his lawyer's name was not found. La Nación's own caution applies: being charged implies neither detention nor demonstrated criminal liability ([La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/)).

## Four companies publish accounts; the rest are known only by their debts

The companies fall into three tiers of evidence. Rated bond issuers have audited statements. A handful of consumer and fintech brands plainly exist but publish nothing. A third set has no visible operating business in any public source and appears only as borrower or conduit. The profiles below follow that order.

### The rated core: real businesses whose profits leaned on the bank

**Ueno Bank** was a branchless digital bank with more than 2.7 million clients, 25,000-plus non-bank correspondents and 926 self-service terminals at December 2025. For fiscal 2025 Solventa&Riskmétrica reported total assets of G. 21,095,542 million, net loans of G. 11,111,835 million, equity of G. 2,117,665 million and net income of G. 313,857 million ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Ueno Bank, cut-off Dec 2025](https://www.ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Informe-de-Calificacion-UENO-BANK-Dic25-firmado_Solventa.pdf)); FIX counted more than 500 bank employees ([FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)). The BCP opened its resolution on 2 November 2026 after adjusting solvency from a reported 13.85% to 5.56% ([5Días special, coverage to 18 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)).

**Grupo Vázquez S.A.E.** is the holding: 28 companies in six business units and more than 6,000 direct employees per Solventa (the group's website says more than 20 companies and 5,500 employees; FIX says more than 5,000) ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Grupo Vázquez, 27 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf); [grupovazquez.com, undated](https://www.grupovazquez.com/nuestra-historia/); [FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)). Its own operating business is cattle: Estancia Don Miguel, about 16,000 hectares with 3,000-plus head sold in 2025, and biological assets of G. 20,128 million ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Grupo Vázquez, 27 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)). The table gives its audited figures. The quality of the 2025 profit is the point to hold on to: individual net income of G. 644,790 million exceeded EBITDA of G. 410,095 million because G. 379,256 million came from valuing investments, while dividends actually received were G. 12,116 million, and 84% of the G. 3,670,282 million of investments in associates was the stake in U Holdings, the bank's Luxembourg parent ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Grupo Vázquez, 27 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)). That carrying value predates the collapse. FIX's group-wide view shows the dependence directly: the bank supplied about 60% of 2025 revenue (roughly US$221 million of US$367 million) and 59% of EBITDA ([FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)). Bonds outstanding stood at G. 828,673 million across six programmes ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Grupo Vázquez, 27 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)); the press reports G. 295,381 million owed to Ueno Bank on an unofficial debtor list ([Última Hora, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)), and FIX cut the issuer from A+py to Cpy on 3 November 2026, citing group debt excluding the bank of US$341 million at June 2026 against about US$71 million of cash ([La Nación, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/crisis-de-ueno-bank-golpea-al-grupo-vazquez-calificadora-rebaja-su-nota-y-advierte-alto-riesgo-de-incumplimiento/)).

**ITTI S.A.E.C.A.** sells hardware, software and technology services to banks, companies and group entities; its Bankitti core-banking product processes more than 40% of Paraguay's financial transactions per FIX, and it reported more than 1,100 corporate clients and 3,184 staff at December 2025 ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on ITTI, 12 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf); [FIX SCR, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)). Revenue rose 80% in 2025. Two details qualify that growth. "Deudores varios", described as mainly inter-company operations, doubled to G. 341,330 million, about 74% of equity by this report's arithmetic, against trade receivables of only G. 62,681 million ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on ITTI, 12 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf)). And La Nación reports FIX as saying **59% of ITTI's revenue came from the bank** when it cut ITTI from A+py to Cpy on 3 November, with total debt of US$66 million at June 2026 ([La Nación, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/caso-ueno-itti-cae-de-a-a-c-en-calificacion-de-riesgo-y-advierten-posible-incumplimiento-de-deudas/)); Solventa flags the same dependence as a risk without quantifying it ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on ITTI, 12 May 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf)). The BCP report, as relayed by Última Hora, calls ITTI the "central operational, strategic and financial node" through which borrowers' loan proceeds passed, an allegation the company has not been reported answering ([Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/itti-el-centro-operativo-por-donde-triangulaban-todos-los-recursos-de-ueno-a-empresas-de-fachada)). Its own reported debt to the bank is modest: G. 86,845 million ([Última Hora, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)).

**Terravaz S.A.E.** (Radice until 2025) is a small real-estate company whose main asset is the Shopping Costanera trust in Encarnación: about 10,000 m² of leasable area, 54 tenants, 93% occupancy, and 48 employees. The mall produced 97.94% of 2025 revenue, so its income comes from tenants and not from the bank ([Solventa&Riskmétrica on Terravaz, 17 Jun 2026, cut-off 31 Dec 2025](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Informe-de-Calificacion-TERRAVAZ-PEG-USD3-Dic2025.pdf)). No credit from Ueno Bank and no role in the case is reported, though Franede's hotel sits inside its mall.

## Conclusion

The three questions turn out to share one answer about evidence. Cadiem, Mauro Vargas Wildberger and most of the borrowers are each visible only through documents produced by someone else: Cadiem through regulator resolutions and investor complaints, Vargas Wildberger through a charging document, the borrowers through a supervisory file leaked in pieces. Where a company speaks for itself through audited accounts, as Grupo Vázquez, ITTI and Terravaz do, the numbers show real activity and a heavy lean on the bank. Where it does not, the only numbers are debts. That makes the income-versus-credit comparison strong for two companies, suggestive for three more and impossible for the rest, and it means the strongest quantitative claims in the case still rest on the prosecution's own tables.

Three events would change this picture quickly. Cadiem's per-fund exposure data and Balanz's due diligence will show whether its loss is a contained write-down or a limits problem. The 18 December hearing is the first point at which Vargas Wildberger's side is likely to be heard. And any administrator's or liquidator's accounts for Franede, AIME and the June borrowers would replace the missing income figures with real ones, which is the single piece of evidence that would most directly test the allegation that these loans never had a repayment source.
