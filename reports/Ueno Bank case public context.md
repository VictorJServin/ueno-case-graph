# Press confirms the loans, not the cash

Public reporting to 20 November 2026 corroborates the documentary spine of Julio Daniel Rey Fernández's statement and leaves its most explosive claims unreported. The related-party lending to Mowi, Grupo Hendy, Grupo M, TUIU, Franede and AIME, the onward routing to ITTI, REDISA, REDIPRO, Vinanzas and Grupo Vázquez, and the 15 June 2026 sale of exactly USD 74,418,418.12 to Vinanzas all appear in press accounts of the Central Bank (BCP) complaint and the charging document, and the five June borrowers' dollar loans as printed by Última Hora add up to the record's USD 81.841 million. Press adds a great deal the record does not carry: a supervisory trail back to August 2025, a second ring of unsecured borrowers, the Luxembourg ownership chain, the removal and detention of President Santiago Peña, charges against the whole former BCP leadership, and a bond that fell to about five cents. It conflicts with the record on three points that matter: outlets date Rey's voluntary statement to 17 or 18 November, not 4 November; OTP Bank invested at one level only, in U Holdings S.à r.l., not through two vehicles; and several names are spelled differently ("Henrici", "Pear", "Dataforge", "Frandede"). No outlet found reports the USD 900 million of deferred expenses, the cash delivery of about USD 17 million plus Gs. 17,000 million, the CDAs endorsed to Miguel Vázquez, Lucca de Barba's authorship of "Delirio Colectivo", or anything on "Kesingtong". Everything below is allegation attributed to the outlet and date cited; no court has ruled on anyone's guilt and every person named is presumed innocent.

### Outlets place Rey's statement two weeks after the record's date

The date is the sharpest conflict. El Nacional (19 Nov) and RDN (20 Nov) both say Rey gave an extended voluntary statement on **Wednesday 18 November 2026**, at his defence's request, and that he is the only former board member to have done so ([El Nacional, 19 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/exdirectivo-ueno-rompe-silencio-destapa-cloaca-piden-reforzar-su-seguridad-n115530); [RDN, 20 Nov 2026](https://rdn.com.py/2026/11/20/exvicepresidente-de-ueno-bank-declaro-ante-la-fiscalia-y-entrego-documentos)). Extra says "el pasado martes", which would be 17 November ([Extra, 20 Nov 2026](https://www.extra.com.py/actualidad/primer-expope-de-ueno-colaboro-dio-detalles-y-documentos-secretos)). The 4 November date does appear in the press, but attached to the charging document (acta de imputación), not to Rey's statement ([La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/)). Two readings fit: the record carries the case-opening date rather than the date he spoke, or Rey gave an earlier statement on 4 November that no outlet has reported. Nothing in the notes settles which, and no outlet says whether the eight bank defendants declared or abstained in early November.

What the press says about content comes almost entirely second-hand through Rey's lawyer, Guillermo Ferreiro. Rey handed over emails, bank documents and reports he says he had asked the board to reject; Ferreiro acknowledged the defence is seeking a cooperation agreement, said the bank had real liquidity problems before the collapse, and said Rey was removed from the board about four months before the resolution ([Extra, 20 Nov 2026](https://www.extra.com.py/actualidad/primer-expope-de-ueno-colaboro-dio-detalles-y-documentos-secretos)). 1000 Noticias reports the claim that some deposits were used to simulate stronger capital backing ([1000 Noticias, 20 Nov 2026](https://1000noticias.com.py/2026/11/20/ueno-exdirectivo-entrego-documentos-sobre-depositos-sospechosos-y-movimientos-millonarios/)). "Delirio Colectivo" is named as an internal report after which two directors who had begun asking questions were removed; no outlet calls it an email, dates it, names its author or names the two directors ([El Nacional, 19 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/exdirectivo-ueno-rompe-silencio-destapa-cloaca-piden-reforzar-su-seguridad-n115530)). The defence asked for reinforced security for Rey in prison; no specific threat is reported.

The one place where an outlet reports Rey's own words to prosecutors in detail is the land deal. ABC Color says Rey told the Fiscalía that on 7 September 2023 the then Financiera Ueno sold about 1,184 hectares in Paraguarí to "Pear S.R.L." for roughly USD 3 million, that the firm "corresponds" to Francisco Peña Palacios, the former president's brother, and that Grupo Vázquez assumed the instalments through forage payments; ABC holds a 3 June 2026 invoice for USD 739,000 and reports no response from those named ([ABC Color, 20 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/20/grupo-vazquez-habria-simulado-creditos-por-venta-de-tierra-a-empresa-de-hermano-de-santiago-pena/)). ABC writes "Pear" in its own voice and "Pearl S.R.L. (sic)" when quoting the statement, so the record's spelling matches the statement and ABC treats it as an error.

### The cash story exists, but from a different source and at a different scale

ABC Color reported on 13 November, from internal emails, messages and spreadsheets supplied by Ueno officials and without citing Rey, that Grupo Vázquez distributed cash in bags and envelopes and that Lucca de Barba, former manager of Financial and Commercial Integration at Grupo Vázquez, coordinated deliveries "por orden de MV". The amounts printed are Gs. 500 million (13 October 2026), Gs. 1,500 million (22 October 2026), Gs. 881 million (2 October 2026) and Gs. 1,176 million (January 2026); no dollar cash figure appears, and ABC reports no response from Vázquez or de Barba ([ABC Color, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/13/grupo-vazquez-distribuia-dinero-efectivo-en-bolsones-y-sobres/)). This independently puts de Barba and cash in the same frame as the record, which is corroboration of theme, not of the record's figures: the largest ABC amount is under a tenth of Gs. 17,000 million. One coincidence deserves a check against the record: ABC's Gs. 500 million entry is dated 13 October 2026, the same day and month as the record's "Delirio Colectivo" email of 13 October 2025. Separately, prosecutors in the Peña case allege USD 200,000 in cash in envelopes found at the presidential residence ([ABC Color, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/13/amplian-imputacion-contra-santiago-en-caso-ueno-bank-pena-incluyen-asociacion-criminal/)), and Extra mentions a list of periodic cash withdrawals shown by Senator Rafael Filizzola that the paper itself calls unconfirmed ([Extra, 20 Nov 2026](https://www.extra.com.py/actualidad/primer-expope-de-ueno-colaboro-dio-detalles-y-documentos-secretos)).

### Name spellings and titles differ across outlets

"Franede" and "Frandede" alternate, sometimes inside one article ([Última Hora, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/fiscalia-imputa-a-los-8-directivos-de-ueno-bank-y-a-otras-19-personas-de-empresas-asociadas)); La Nación's list of the 27 uses "FRANDEDE S.A." The record's "Henrichi" is "Henrici S.A." in Última Hora. "Data Force" has no match, while "Dataforge S.A." is a member of a consortium awarded a quiniela (numbers game) concession in 2026. "U Luxemburgo" appears nowhere; the Luxembourg entity is U Holdings S.à r.l. Prosecutor Silvio Corbeta is also printed "Corbetta", ITTI appears as both S.A.E.C.A. and S.A.E., and the fund manager as "AFPISA". Rey's own title is consistent, former vicepresidente segundo, but Miguel Vázquez is "vicepresidente primero" in La Nación and Telefuturo, "ex-CEO and ex-2nd VP" in ABC (5 Nov) and "exdirector ejecutivo" in El Nacional (10 Nov) ([La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/); [ABC Color, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/05/caso-ueno-juez-envia-a-prision-a-miguel-vazquez-y-principales-exdirectivos/); [El Nacional, 10 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/caso-ueno-agravan-cargos-miguel-vazquez-ratifican-su-prision-n115044)).

## A solvency ratio of 5.56% ended a bank with 1.8 million funded accounts

The record says nothing about how the bank fell. The press supplies it. The BCP board voted unanimously on 2 November 2026 to open a resolution under Law 2.334/03 and announced it in the early hours of 3 November ([La Nación, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/el-bcp-dispone-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/); [ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-bcp-anuncia-procedimiento-de-resolucion/)); some outlets therefore date the resolution to 3 November. The stated cause was a solvency ratio under half the legal minimum: **13.85% reported, 12% required, 5.56% after supervisory adjustments**, with a provisions deficit of G. 1.62 trillion ([5Días special, coverage to 18 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)). Independiente gives the adjustment as Gs. 1,621,853,458,972 and cautions that it is not, by itself, a proven loss ([Independiente](https://independiente.com.py/caso-ueno-bank-fiscalia-imputa-a-miguel-vazquez-y-otras-26-personas-por-presunto-esquema-de-operaciones-financieras-irregulares/)). A banking executive's reference to a "US$1,600 million" capital deficit ([ABC Color, 19 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/19/ejecutivo-financiero-detalla-las-omisiones-que-permitieron-el-colapso-de-ueno-bank/)) looks like a unit confusion with G. 1.6 trillion and should not be relied on.

Ueno had grown from a finance company bought by Grupo Vázquez in 2021 into a bank licensed on 22 November 2023, and absorbed Visión Banco in June 2024 ([Forbes Argentina, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/de-financiera-banco-tres-millones-clientes-ueno-entidad-intervenida-paraguay-n101669)). It claimed about 3 million customers; the BCP found 1,824,994 accounts with a balance, against deposits of about USD 2.8 billion ([AFP via Emol, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2026/11/11/1216757/ueno-bank-devolucion-dinero-banco.html)).

The supervisory trail gives the record's 15 June transaction its context. A Superintendency of Banks (SIB) report of 29 August 2025 found no adequate accounting separation for the past-due portfolio covered by the merger facility ([ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)). On 5 June 2026 the SIB confirmed Mowi and Grupo Hendy as related parties and notified an excess; the BCP later said the excess was "corrected" on 15 June, the day of the Vinanzas sale ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas); [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)). An SIB findings report of 5 August 2026 concluded the bank had indirectly financed the purchase of its own impaired portfolio ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)). On 14 October 2026 the bank denied being under a regularisation plan, yet the BCP's 3 November communiqué attributes the fatal adjustments to "el proceso de regularización" ([5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno); [La Nación, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/el-bcp-dispone-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/)); no source reconciles the two.

### The deferred-expense claim has public cousins, none at USD 900 million

Rey's USD 900 million figure is not in the press, but the mechanism it points to is. ABC Color, citing an internal BCP memo of 29 June 2026 that it did not independently verify, reports that the BCP board authorised two facilities for the Visión merger on 27 May 2024 totalling **G. 5.1 trillion (about USD 691 million)**: an expenses-and-investments trust of G. 2.5 trillion (about USD 335 million) booked as deferred charges, and a past-due loan facility of G. 2.6 trillion (about USD 356 million). A USD 20 million addendum of 13 March 2025 fell, per the memo, outside permitted expenses, and suppliers paid from the trust included ITTI and Ueno Holding ([ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)). A specialist outlet reports that trust-related rights of about USD 353 million equalled 99.4% of reported equity at end-2025, under accounts PwC Paraguay signed without qualification on 19 February 2026; it carries no PwC statement and says the published material does not establish what the auditor knew ([Big4News, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.big4news.com/p/pwc-ueno-bank-collapse-audit-accounting)). Whether Rey's USD 900 million combines these items with others cannot be determined from the press.

### Payouts moved fast; the transfer has not happened

An emergency law promulgated on 4 November allowed an advance of up to 40 minimum wages (G. 121.76 million) per depositor; by the close of 9 November G. 2.126 trillion had gone to 1,738,804 depositors ([5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)), and by 12 November about G. 3.9 trillion to more than 1.8 million ([Global Finance, 12 Nov 2026](https://gfmag.com/banking/ueno-failure-to-cost-paraguay-central-bank-347m/)). Moody's estimated 97% of depositors but only 30% of deposit value inside the guarantee ([5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)), so the residual risk sits with institutions. Public-sector exposure is printed anywhere from USD 800 million to almost USD 900 million depending on outlet and date, and the IPS (social security institute) figure ranges from about USD 345 million ([Revista Foco, 3 Nov 2026](https://revistafoco.com.py/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-alcance-de-la-resolucion-y-los-proximos-pasos-del-bcp/)) to USD 516 million ([AFP via Emol, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2026/11/11/1216757/ueno-bank-devolucion-dinero-banco.html)). Estimates of the guarantee fund's cost also conflict: about USD 740 million (AFP) against up to USD 928 million ([Global Finance, 12 Nov 2026](https://gfmag.com/banking/ueno-failure-to-cost-paraguay-central-bank-347m/)). On 18 November the BCP said assets suffice to cover all deposits and postponed the exclusion balance by 15 days; six days earlier it had estimated healthy assets at USD 600 million ([5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)). As of 19 November no assets or deposits had been transferred and no acquirer named ([ABC Color, 19 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/19/resolucion-de-ueno-bank-el-bcp-prepara-paquetes-de-activos-y-depositos-para-traspasar-a-otras-entidades/)).

## Twenty-seven charged, ten detained, and an appeals court that added laundering

The press matches the record's case number and caption: Expediente 89/2026, "Federico Miguel Vázquez Villasanti y otros s/ Lesión de Confianza y otros" ([La Nación, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/04/caso-ueno-bank-imputan-a-27-personas-y-piden-prision-de-miguel-vazquez/)). The 27 comprise eight from the bank and 19 linked to the borrowers. La Nación lists the bank defendants as Vázquez, Juan Manuel Gustale Cardoni (president), **Rey (former vicepresidente segundo)**, Diana Mongelós, Mariana Torres Rubiani, Alejandro Gómez Abente, Diego Duarte Schussmuller and Miguel Ángel Almada Frutos (former síndico), and stresses that being charged implies neither detention nor proven liability ([La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/)). The 19 are officers or shareholders of Mowi, Grupo Hendy, Grupo M, TUIU, Franede and AIME, plus ITTI's César Astigarraga; Última Hora's own text names only 17 of them ([Última Hora, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/fiscalia-imputa-a-los-8-directivos-de-ueno-bank-y-a-otras-19-personas-de-empresas-asociadas)). No officer of Vinanzas, SATEC, HD Invest, Technoma, La Tropa Fletes or Factopar appears among the 27 by that affiliation. Prosecutors later spoke of about 35 people and 37 companies linked to the fund movements, without charging all of them ([La Nación, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/13/caso-ueno-quienes-son-los-27-imputados-por-las-presuntas-irregularidades-financieras-del-banco/)).

The original four counts were lesión de confianza (breach of trust), improper conduct in crisis situations, attempted fraud and breach of bookkeeping duties. On 10 November the specialised Appeals Tribunal, of its own motion, added money laundering and criminal association and recast the fraud count as consummated aggravated fraud ([El Nacional, 10 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/caso-ueno-agravan-cargos-miguel-vazquez-ratifican-su-prision-n115044); [Última Hora, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/caso-ueno-defensa-de-gustale-recurre-a-la-corte-contra-la-resolucion-que-amplia-los-cargos)). Gustale's lawyers took that to the Supreme Court, arguing the tribunal acted as accuser rather than reviewer and that nobody, not even the Fiscalía, had appealed the legal classification ([Última Hora, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/caso-ueno-defensa-de-gustale-recurre-a-la-corte-contra-la-resolucion-que-amplia-los-cargos)); Vázquez's new lawyer also appealed and said he had not yet seen the charging document ([ADN Digital, 16 Nov 2026](https://www.adndigital.com.py/abogado-de-vazquez-apelo-decision-de-ampliar-acusacion-contra-su-cliente/)). Outlets apply the expansion inconsistently: ABC lists it for all eight on 16 November, then describes Astigarraga's charges as the original four on 19 November. No ruling from the Supreme Court is reported.

On custody, Judge Rodrigo Estigarribia went beyond the prosecution's request. The Fiscalía asked for prison only for Vázquez; on 5 November the judge sent six to pre-trial prison, **Rey among them**, and two to house arrest ([ABC Color, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/05/caso-ueno-juez-envia-a-prision-a-miguel-vazquez-y-principales-exdirectivos/)). Astigarraga surrendered on 12 November after a week as a fugitive and his appeal was held inadmissible on 19 November ([ABC Color, 19 Nov 2026](https://abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/19/caso-ueno-descartan-recurso-y-cesar-astigarraga-seguira-con-prision-preventiva)). ABC counted ten people deprived of liberty in Case 89/2026 on 16 November, including a Ueno Casa de Bolsa operations chief charged separately with obstruction over alleged document destruction; the other 18 company-linked defendants were at liberty ([ABC Color, 16 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/16/uno-a-uno-la-situacion-de-todos-los-detenidos-por-el-caso-ueno-bank/)). A civil court, at the BCP's request, ordered a preventive embargo of USD 70 million plus G. 1.6 trillion against U Paraguay S.A., U Holdings S.à r.l., Grupo Vázquez S.A.E., the beneficial owner and six directors ([Última Hora, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/embargan-por-usd-70-millones-los-bienes-de-directivos-de-ueno-bank-y-del-grupo-vazquez)), and seizures included nine vehicles, a yacht and two aircraft ([EFE via El Mostrador, 8 Nov 2026](https://www.elmostrador.cl/noticias/2026/11/08/autos-de-lujo-avionetas-un-yate-y-documentos-las-incautaciones-en-caso-ueno-en-paraguay/)).

Rey is the only bank defendant whose side has addressed the substance in public. Ferreiro's line is that Rey "asumirá la responsabilidad por lo que tenga que asumir, por lo que hizo, pero no por lo que no hizo" ([El Nacional, 19 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/exdirectivo-ueno-rompe-silencio-destapa-cloaca-piden-reforzar-su-seguridad-n115530)). The others have argued procedure only. No post-collapse statement on the merits from Vázquez was found; Última Hora published an undated pre-collapse staff call in which he said nothing would happen to the bank or to clients' money ([Última Hora, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/miguel-vazquez-antes-de-caida-de-ueno-al-banco-no-le-va-a-pasar-nada-el-dinero-esta-ultra-blindado)). One point bears on how to read Rey: a rating report with a 31 December 2025 cut-off lists him as a director of Grupo Vázquez S.A.E. alongside Gustale ([Solventa&Riskmétrica via BVA, 27 May 2026](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)), and his lawyer says he moved to another group company after leaving the bank's board. He is an insider seeking a cooperation deal, which is a reason to weigh his uncorroborated claims with care.

## Vinanzas held USD 93,828 the day before it paid USD 74.4 million

This is where press and record align most closely, because both draw on the BCP's 199-page file sent to the Fiscal General on 3 November ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)).

On the Vinanzas balance, Última Hora reports that the company had **USD 93,828 in its account on 14 June 2026**, received USD 76,061,604.13 on 15 June and paid Ueno USD 74,418,418.12 in cash for impaired loans of Mowi, Grupo Hendy and Grupo M ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)). Any balance figure in the record should be checked against those three numbers. Note that the portfolio sold covers three borrowers only; TUIU, Franede and AIME are reported as borrowers with presumed irregular credits, not as part of the portfolio ([Economis, 5 Nov 2026](https://economis.com.ar/ueno-bank-investigan-maniobra-de-us-74-millones-en-la-que-el-banco-habria-comprado-sus-propios-creditos/)).

On the funding loans, the dollar amounts Última Hora prints for the record's five borrowers are SATEC USD 20 million, HD Invest USD 35 million, Factopar USD 6.841 million, Technoma USD 10 million and La Tropa Fletes USD 10 million ([Última Hora, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos); [Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/itti-el-centro-operativo-por-donde-triangulaban-todos-los-recursos-de-ueno-a-empresas-de-fachada); [Última Hora, 16 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/ganadera-la-tropa-triangulo-prestamos-del-grupo-vazquez-y-movio-animales-en-plena-intervencion)). Those sum to USD 81.841 million, the record's figure; the addition is this report's arithmetic, not a total any outlet prints. The press then departs from the record in four ways. First, Última Hora dates the Technoma and La Tropa Fletes loans to **11 June**, not 15 June. Second, those two loans did not go straight to Vinanzas: they bought bonds of ITTI, Grupo Vázquez, Kasas Propiedades and Model S.A., partly through Ueno Casa de Bolsa, and the issuers forwarded the proceeds. Third, there were guaraní loans the same day that the record's dollar total omits: HD Invest G. 36,900 million, Factopar G. 93,000 million and G. 64,500 million, and Sagas S.A. G. 86,100 million. Fourth, ITTI sent Vinanzas USD 4,678,670 plus G. 31,200 million directly. Outlets also differ among themselves: Hoy's summary of the BCP complaint names only Satec and HD Invest ([Hoy, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/04/timba-con-dinero-publico-denuncia-del-bcp-revela-perverso-esquema-de-ueno)), and Economis lists Kasas (USD 10 million), ITTI, Grupo Vázquez and Model as flows while warning they should not be summed ([Economis, 5 Nov 2026](https://economis.com.ar/ueno-bank-investigan-maniobra-de-us-74-millones-en-la-que-el-banco-habria-comprado-sus-propios-creditos/)). Part of what Vinanzas received went back out the same day: G. 110,940 million to REDISA and G. 18,770 million to REDIPRO, which paid down Hendy and Mowi overdrafts ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)).

### The borrowers share officers with the group

The record's Gs. 1.4 trillion total is neither confirmed nor contradicted. The firmest public figure is G. 743,784 million (about USD 123 million) for Mowi, Grupo Hendy and Grupo M at 30 April 2026, equal to 30% of effective equity against a 20% cap, from a 10 July 2026 report by then BCP supervision manager Fernando Rivarola ([ABC Color, 10 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/10/solo-tres-empresas-vinculadas-a-ueno-tenian-us-415-millones-por-encima-del-tope-legal/)); ABC five days later cited about USD 146 million and 35% for the same three ([ABC Color, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/abc-color/2026/10/15/ueno-bank-modifica-sus-acciones-para-evitar-deducciones-pero-contradice-a-conacom/)). Individual disbursements are printed for each borrower, for example USD 24.9 million over ten years to Franede on 28 August 2025 and G. 133,000 million plus USD 6.8 million to AIME in January 2026 ([Última Hora, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/cronologia-de-la-caida-de-ueno-el-bcp-dejo-operar-10-meses-al-banco-con-creditos-a-vinculadas)), but no outlet totals TUIU, Franede or AIME.

The press adds who stands behind the names. Mowi was incorporated in 2021 with Grupo Vázquez holding all but one share; in September 2025 the shares passed to José Augusto Sala Cardozo, a former Ueno manager, and Sergio Pizurno ([El Observador, 4 Nov 2026](https://observador.com.py/ueno-dilapido-dinero-del-publico-en-prestamos-sin-garantias-a-sus-empresas-vinculadas/)). ITTI notified the competition authority in 2024 of acquiring 55% of Grupo Hendy and 50% of Grupo M, owner of the Pagopar payment platform ([ABC Color, 8 Sep 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/09/08/ueno-bank-misteriosas-empresas-citadas-en-proyecto-de-pedido-de-informe-estan-ligadas-al-grupo-vazquez/)). The SIB's conclusion, as reported, was a single risk unit under common management, with twelve home-banking operators of the borrowers being active ITTI employees ([Última Hora, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/itti-el-centro-operativo-por-donde-triangulaban-todos-los-recursos-de-ueno-a-empresas-de-fachada)). ITTI, of which Grupo Vázquez holds 88.80% ([Solventa&Riskmétrica via BVA, 27 May 2026](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)), drew 59% of its revenue from the bank and was cut by FIX SCR from A+py to Cpy on 3 November ([La Nación, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/caso-ueno-itti-cae-de-a-a-c-en-calificacion-de-riesgo-y-advierten-posible-incumplimiento-de-deudas/)).

### A second ring of borrowers the record barely touches

Última Hora reports that the charging document sorts a further group into a "Bloque C" of firms showing the same pattern of unsecured loans beyond capacity but no indications of links to the scheme: GSR E.A.S., Nuevo Mundo Entertainment, Nubicom, Office Design, Grupo Berilo and Galadriel ([Última Hora, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/ueno-presto-usd-78-millones-a-gsr-firma-con-capital-de-g-100-millones-representada-por-su-exgerente)). The paper questions that classification for GSR, which owed about USD 78 million on paid-in capital of G. 100 million and whose registered legal representative is Sala Cardozo, the charged Mowi shareholder. The record's "Henrichi" fits here: **Henrici S.A.** borrowed USD 10.593 million on 27 March 2026, passed it to ITTI, and carries a balance of G. 345,372 million on an unofficial debtor list ([Última Hora, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)). No owners or business line for Henrici were found. A judge froze the accounts of ten more companies on 6 November, among them SATEC, HD Invest, Technoma, La Tropa Fletes, Factopar and Kasas ([ABC Color, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/06/caso-ueno-juez-bloquea-bienes-de-otras-empresas-vinculadas-al-grupo-vazquez/)).

## A president removed in four days and a regulator charged in ten

None of this is in a record dated 4 November. The account below gives the prosecution and congressional case and the defence answer in turn.

**The case against.** The Chamber of Deputies received an impeachment accusation on 4 November; Colorado Party leader Horacio Cartes announced on 5 November that the party would back it; Peña tendered a resignation the Senate declined to process; and on 6 November the Senate removed him for poor performance of duties with 44 votes, after which Vice-President Pedro Alliana was sworn in ([Infobae, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/america/america-latina/2026/11/06/el-congreso-de-paraguay-destituyo-a-santiago-pena-por-su-vinculo-con-el-colapso-de-ueno-bank/); [ABC Color, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/06/en-vivo-juicio-politico-a-pena-senado-retoma-proceso-en-medio-de-renuncia-trabada-y-pedido-de-prorroga/)). Early reports say "resigned" and later ones "removed"; both are accurate to their moment. In a separate file, Case 92/2026, a judge ordered preventive detention on 7 November on charges of breach of trust as instigator and administration for personal benefit ([Infobae, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/america/america-latina/2026/11/07/la-fiscalia-de-paraguay-pidio-la-prision-preventiva-del-ex-presidente-santiago-pena-por-el-colapso-del-ueno-bank/)). On 13 November prosecutors added criminal association, alleging he acted as head of the structure, met Vázquez privately, ordered that the bank not be intervened and received illicit payments ([ABC Color, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/13/amplian-imputacion-contra-santiago-en-caso-ueno-bank-pena-incluyen-asociacion-criminal/)). Prosecutors put total damage at USD 809.5 million ([MercoPress, 9 Nov 2026](https://es.mercopress.com/2026/11/09/hallan-una-boveda-blindada-oculta-en-una-casa-de-pena-construida-con-prestamos-de-ueno-bank)). The accusation rests heavily on the growth of state money in the bank: from USD 80.2 million in 2023 to USD 834.8 million in September 2026, about USD 355 million of it from the IPS ([BBC Mundo via El Observador CR, 10 Nov 2026](https://observador.cr/los-millones-de-paraguayos-afectados-por-la-crisis-del-ueno-bank-que-aun-esperan-la-devolucion-de-sus-ahorros/)), after the IPS council loosened its placement rules in November 2023 and November 2024 ([ABC Color, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/07/08/cambios-clave-en-el-ips-coincidieron-con-la-catarata-de-fondos-para-ueno-bank/)).

**The defence.** Peña declares himself innocent and declined to testify. His lawyer Ricardo Preda told the Senate the accusation rests on "indicios", that no act or file signed by Peña favouring the bank is identified, that the alleged scheme was built after Peña left the company, that the BCP itself detected the irregularities and filed the complaint, which he says is inconsistent with a captured regulator, and that the Senate approved the BCP president's appointment ([Última Hora, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/preda-dice-que-el-libelo-no-tiene-pruebas-y-culpa-al-congreso-por-nombrar-a-carvallo)). Preda also says Peña never held shares in the bank itself, only an interest in Ueno Holding dating from Credicentro that was bought out and audited by the Comptroller, and that a president does not tell agencies where to bank ([1000 Noticias, 7 Nov 2026](https://1000noticias.com.py/2026/11/07/defensa-de-pena-considera-adecuada-la-reclusion-en-la-escolta-presidencial-y-augura-su-sobreseimiento/)). The exit date is unsettled even on the defence side: March 2025 in the accusation, April 2025 and "late 2024 or early 2025" from Preda. No defence response was found to the 13 November charge or the cash allegation. Readers should also know that ABC Color used labels such as "banco amigo de Santi Peña" well before the collapse; those are the paper's characterisations, and the procurement stories below are almost entirely its work.

**The regulator.** By 13 November the whole former BCP leadership was charged with breach of trust and criminal association: former president Carlos Carvallo, who had chaired Financiera Ueno until August 2023, Superintendent Holger Insfrán, and directors Marco Aurelio González, Liana Caballero and Miguel Mora ([ABC Color, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/13/amplian-imputacion-contra-santiago-en-caso-ueno-bank-pena-incluyen-asociacion-criminal/); [La Política Online, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/justicia-py/detienen-a-mora-liana-y-marco-aurelio-y-toda-la-ex-cupula-del-bcp-ya-esta-imputada/)). Prosecutors allege they knew of the insolvency from at least December 2025 and that a technical report was altered before a Senate appearance on 9 October 2026; Insfrán is alleged to have withheld the Rivarola memo of 10 July 2026. Coverage is almost wholly prosecution-sourced. Carvallo declined to testify and said he fears for his life ([ABC Color, 8 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/08/caso-ueno-bank-expresidente-del-bcp-afirma-que-teme-por-su-vida/)); AP reported the regulator's position that he had recused himself from Ueno decisions ([AP via KSAT, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/03/paraguay-seizes-major-digital-bank-tied-to-president-leaving-millions-of-customers-in-limbo/)). No statements from the other four were found. This touches Rey directly: his lawyer says Rey knew of internal reports urging corrective measures but did not know the BCP's "informe Rivarola" ([El Nacional, 19 Nov 2026](https://elnacional.com.py/politica/exdirectivo-ueno-rompe-silencio-destapa-cloaca-piden-reforzar-su-seguridad-n115530)). Attorney General Emiliano Rolón resigned on 17 November under an impeachment motion alleging delay ([Diario Siglo XXI, agencies, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.diariosigloxxi.com/texto-ep/mostrar/20261117194632/dimite-fiscal-general-paraguay-escandalo-ueno-bank)).

The record's mention of COMITIA has a public counterpart that is thinner than the name suggests. Consorcio Comitia-MSA was the sole bidder for an electoral tribunal lease of 18,000 voting machines worth about USD 35 million. The reported link to Ueno is indirect: the consortium was represented until September 2025 by a law firm whose principal sat on Ueno Bank's board, and its authorised-persons list included two lawyers with Ueno or Grupo Vázquez ties ([ABC Color, 22 May 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/05/22/proveedora-del-tsje-expuso-nexos-con-ueno-bank-en-la-primera-fallida-licitacion/)). No article places ITTI inside the consortium. For "Data Force", the nearest match is Dataforge S.A., one of three members of a consortium awarded a quiniela concession by Conajzar in April 2026 ([Hoy, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/04/20/conajzar-anuncia-dos-adjudicaciones-de-la-quiniela)); no source found says who owns it or links it to Grupo Vázquez. What prosecutors did seize at Vázquez's home, per ABC, was a spreadsheet described as a plan for ITTI to win about USD 241 million of state tenders; voting machines and the quiniela are not on it ([ABC Color, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/04/hallazgo-fiscal-en-casa-de-miguel-vazquez-tenia-un-libreto-para-ganar-licitaciones-multimillonarias/)).

## OTP bought 9.99% of one Luxembourg company and under 1% of the votes

The record's two-vehicle description of OTP's investment does not match the published structure. Última Hora, working from the Luxembourg registry, gives the chain as Ueno Bank, 93.05% held by U Paraguay S.A., which is wholly owned by U Holdings S.à r.l., in which Grupo Vázquez has 86.73% of shares and 98.82% of votes and **OTP Bank 9.99% of shares and 0.89% of votes** ([Última Hora, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/la-conexion-luxemburgo-dentro-del-entramado-del-caso-ueno-bank)). Paperjam reports the same percentages and says Grupo Vázquez obtained its voting control through five million profit-sharing shares subscribed for USD 50,000 ([Paperjam, undated, viewed 20 Nov 2026](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/ueno-bank-the-paraguayan-bankruptcy-that-leads-to-luxembourg)). "U Luxemburgo" is most plausibly Rey's shorthand for U Holdings, and "U Paraguay" the intermediate company OTP did not buy into; that reading is this report's inference.

The amount is well documented: USD 30 million in December 2025 ([RDN, 23 Dec 2025](https://rdn.com.py/2025/12/23/otp-bank-expands-into-paraguay-through-ueno-bank-investment/)) and a further USD 17 million announced on 3 June 2026, for USD 47 million in total ([ueno.com.py, 3 Jun 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/?p=13476)). Two timing conflicts remain. Bloomberg says the stake reached nearly 10% "in August" ([Bloomberg Línea, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/escandalo-de-ueno-bank-pone-en-duda-la-reputacion-de-paraguay-como-destino-de-inversion/)), and Última Hora describes OTP's entry as consolidated in mid-2026 with USD 30 million. Ueno's June release called OTP its "socio principal", which the Luxembourg filings contradict. After the collapse OTP said it was a purely financial investor with no control rights ([Telex, 3 Nov 2026](https://telex.hu/gazdasag/2026/11/03/bedolt-ueno-bank-paraguay-otp-bank-befektetes)), booked a USD 32 million negative fair-value adjustment in its third-quarter report, and its deputy chief executive László Bencsik said neither OTP nor the regulator knew of the problems and that due diligence was lighter than for a full acquisition ([Világgazdaság, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.vg.hu/penz-es-tokepiac/2026/11/otp-bank-a-holtterbol-ugrott-elo-a-paraguayi-bankcsod)). OTP later told EFE the problems could not have been foreseen ([EFE via Unitel, 19 Nov 2026](https://unitel.bo/noticias/agencias/el-otp-bank-hungaro-defiende-haber-invertido-en-el-ueno-y-habla-de-problemas-imprevisibles-KO24916590)). Paperjam also reports that U Holdings carried U Paraguay at USD 482.4 million against equity of USD 225.3 million, and that shares were distributed to 22 new holders in March 2024; Última Hora names the former president's brother among 2024 holders who are absent by 2026.

Other foreign money is confirmed. Ueno issued USD 350 million of five-year 6.70% notes in March 2026 ([ueno.com.py, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/en/2026/03/09/ueno-bank-us-350-million-senior-notes-the-largest-ever-by-a-paraguayan-bank/)); they began November above 85 cents and fell to about 5 ([Bloomberg Línea, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/escandalo-de-ueno-bank-pone-en-duda-la-reputacion-de-paraguay-como-destino-de-inversion/)). IDB Invest lent about USD 20 million in guaraníes in 2024 ([IDB Invest, 18 Nov 2024](https://www.idbinvest.org/en/news-media/idb-invest-and-ueno-bank-join-forces-promote-financial-inclusion-paraguay)), and Triodos Bank and GAWA Capital USD 17.5 million in August 2026 ([ueno.com.py, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/?p=15302)). One politically oriented outlet names the bookrunners, lists bondholders and asserts that funds are preparing a New York suit and that US agencies are looking at the sale ([La Política Online, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/economia-py/los-fondos-internacionales-preparan-una-demanda-por-los-bonos-basura-de-ueno-bank/)); no wire corroborates the US-agency claim and no suit is confirmed as filed. ## What could not be confirmed

Six elements of the record have no public counterpart in the notes: the USD 900 million of deferred expenses; the cash delivery of about USD 17 million plus Gs. 17,000 million; CDAs endorsed to Miguel Vázquez; the description of "Delirio Colectivo" as an email, its 13 October 2025 date and Lucca de Barba as its author; any entity called "Kesingtong" in any spelling; and a statement by Rey dated 4 November. A targeted search also found no reporting on McKinsey work for Ueno. Absence from the press is not evidence against the record; these are simply claims that rest on Rey alone for now.

Several record points are confirmed only in part. The Gs. 1.4 trillion total for the six borrowers has no printed equivalent, and no outlet totals TUIU, Franede or AIME. The USD 81.841 million matches only by adding figures from three Última Hora articles. "Data Force" as Dataforge S.A. is a name match without any reported link to the group. COMITIA's link is through shared lawyers and is contested. No source places ITTI in the Comitia consortium.

On the wider case, the following stayed open at 20 November. The BCP resolution number was not seen on any BCP page; the two research strands disagree on whether La Nación prints "Resolución N.º 1". No shareholders or officers were found for Vinanzas, SATEC, HD Invest, Factopar, Kasas, Model, Henrici or Grupo Berilo, and no response from Vinanzas, de Barba or the 18 company-linked defendants at liberty. The names of the BCP interveners, any acquirer or tender date, and the treatment of balances above the guarantee are unreported. Who led the BCP between 3 and 9 November is printed three different ways. Whether Grupo Vázquez met bond interest falling due from 19 November is unknown. No foreign freeze, no bondholder suit, no post-collapse statement from IDB Invest, Triodos, GAWA or PwC, and no Supreme Court ruling on the expanded charges were found. The S&P downgrade to D rests on a Wikipedia entry and the Fitch downgrade on one undated mention.

Finally, a caution on the sources themselves. Researchers read the pages through an automated summarising tool, so Spanish quotations and exact figures should be re-checked against the originals before reuse. The BCP's and the Ministerio Público's own releases were not retrieved; everything here is press. Items the researchers saw only as headlines have been left out of this report.

## Conclusion

The press record changes how Rey's statement should be read in two ways. First, the parts of it that overlap with the BCP file are no longer his word alone: the borrowers, the routing, the June sale and its financing are in a regulator's complaint that predates his cooperation, and the press prints the same numbers. That lifts the credibility of the ledger and leaves the cash, the CDAs and the USD 900 million standing on a single interested witness who was a director of both the bank and the holding and is negotiating a deal. The partial overlap with ABC's independently sourced cash story, de Barba included, is the strongest outside signal that those claims are worth testing, and the gap in scale is the reason not to treat them as tested.

Second, the record is a narrower document than the case has become. It describes a related-party lending scheme inside one bank; by 20 November the public case had a second file with a former president and five former central bankers as defendants, an appeals court adding laundering and criminal association on its own motion, and a Luxembourg holding whose valuation and vanished minority shareholders are now part of the story. The points most likely to move next are the Supreme Court's answer on the expanded charges, the first foreign freeze, and whether prosecutors or the court characterise Rey's statement in public, which would settle its date.
