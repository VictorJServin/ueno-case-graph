## 1. Código Penal (Ley 1160/97; amended by Ley 3440/08 and Ley 6452/19)

Sources (all primary):
- Ley 1160/97, BACN page, promulgated 1997-11-26. The page gives no amendment notes; treat it as the 1997 text. https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/3497/ley-n-1160--codigo--penal
- Ley 3440/08, BACN, promulgated 2008-07-16, published 2008-08-20. Of the articles of interest it amends Arts. 26, 181, 192 and 196; it does **not** amend Arts. 16, 27, 29, 178, 179, 187 or 239. Its Art. 3: in force one year after promulgation, except Arts. 104, 148, 154, 165, 181, 182 and 316 (in force the day after publication). https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/3485/ley-n-3440-modifica-varias-disposiciones-de-la-ley-n-116097-codigo-penal
- Ley 6452/19, BACN, promulgated 2019-11-29, published 2019-12-02. Amends Arts. 91 and 196 and adds Arts. 191a, 191b, 268a, 268b, 268c. https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9058/ley-n-6452-modifica-varias-disposiciones-de-la-ley-n-11601997-codigo-penal-y-su-modificatoria-la-ley-n-34402008 ; same text in a private PDF copy: https://www.maxicambios.com.py/storage/app/media/DOCUMENTOS%20Y%20SOLICITUDES/CUMPLIMIENTO/LEY%206452-2019.pdf

### General part

**Art. 13 (crimen / delito).** Crimes are punishable acts whose statutory sanction is prison of more than five years; delitos are those with prison of up to five years or a fine. Only the frame of the base offence is considered. Fragment: "pena privativa de libertad mayor de cinco años".

**Art. 14 inc. 1 núm. 4 (hecho antijurídico).** Conduct that fulfils the legal type and is not justified. Fragment: "la conducta que cumpla con los presupuestos del tipo legal".

**Art. 16 (actuación en representación de otro).** (1) A natural person who acts as representative of a legal person or member of its organs, as managing partner of a partnership, or as legal representative of another, answers personally for the punishable act even if the special personal conditions required by the type are present only in the represented entity. (2) The same applies to a person appointed by the owner of a business as its manager ("encargado") or expressly charged with specific duties of the owner under own responsibility. (3) Extends to mandates from entities performing public-administration tasks. (4) Applies even if the legal act founding the representation is invalid. Fragment: "responderá personalmente por el hecho punible". No penalty of its own.

**Art. 26 (tentativa, text of Ley 3440/08).** There is attempt when the author carries out the decision to commit a punishable act through acts which, on the author's own picture of the facts, immediately precede the completion of the type. Fragment: "Hay tentativa cuando el autor ejecutara la decisión".

**Art. 27 (punibilidad de la tentativa).** (1) Attempt of crimes is punishable; attempt of delitos only where the law says so expressly. (2) The frame of the completed offence applies. (3) If the author has not yet done everything needed for completion (unfinished attempt), the penalty is mitigated under Art. 67. Fragment: "La tentativa de los crímenes es punible".

**Art. 28 (desistimiento).** Voluntary abandonment, or preventing the result, exempts from penalty. Fragment: "quedará eximido de pena el que voluntariamente desista".

**Art. 29 (autoría).** Inc. 1: whoever carries out the act personally or by using another is punished as author. Inc. 2: likewise whoever acts in agreement with another so that his contribution gives him shared control over the execution (co-authorship). Fragment: "Será castigado como autor el que realizara el hecho". The record invokes inc. 2.

**Art. 30 (instigación).** Inducing another to an intentional unlawful act; same penalty as the author. Fragment: "Será castigado como instigador el que induzca a otro".

**Art. 31 (complicidad).** Helping another to commit an intentional unlawful act; author's penalty mitigated under Art. 67. Fragment: "Será castigado como cómplice el que ayudara a otro".

**Art. 32.** If the instigator or accomplice lacks the special personal qualities (Art. 16) that found the author's liability, the penalty is mitigated under Art. 67.

**Arts. 38, 52, 67, 70.** Prison lasts from six months to twenty-five years (Art. 38: "una duración mínima de seis meses"). Fine: 5 to 360 day-fines unless the law says otherwise (Art. 52). Art. 67 mitigation: maximum cut to three quarters; minimum cut to two years where it was five or ten, to one year where it was two or three, otherwise to the legal minimum ("no podrá exceder las tres cuartas partes"). Art. 70 concurrence: one single penalty fixed on the gravest frame, which may be raised by up to half of that frame's maximum, within Art. 38 limits ("será condenado a una sola pena").

### Special part

**Art. 178 (conducta conducente a la quiebra)** (1997 text; not amended by Ley 3440). Inc. 1: up to five years or fine for whoever (1) founds or expands a business on a clearly insufficient capital base ("con una base de capital claramente insuficiente"); (2) buys goods or securities on credit and disposes of them considerably below value; (3) runs a business that must keep commercial books without obtaining, through proper bookkeeping or otherwise, knowledge of its real position. Inc. 2 (objective condition of punishability): punishable only if the author, or the business, has ceased payments or been declared bankrupt, and a link between the conduct and that outcome cannot be excluded ("no se pueda excluir una conexión"). Press reports (ABC, 16 Nov 2026) say this offence was added for one defendant after the appeals ruling; see `research_notes/.../criminal_case.md`.

**Art. 179 (conducta indebida en situaciones de crisis)** (1997 text). Chapeau: "En caso de insolvencia o iliquidez inminente o acontecida". Up to five years or fine for whoever, in that situation: (1) spends or commits to exaggerated sums through loss-making or speculative deals, gambling or difference transactions; (2) reduces through other legal transactions the assets that would fall into the bankruptcy estate; (3) removes or hides such assets; (4) feigns rights of others or acknowledges sham rights ("simulara derechos de otros"); (5) removes, hides, destroys or damages commercial books or papers before the retention period ends; (6) contrary to law (a) draws up or alters balance sheets so that the real patrimonial position is hard to know ("elaborara o modificara balances"), or (b) fails to draw up the balance sheet or inventory in time; (7) uses false or distorted summaries of the state of the business in commerce. Inc. 2: up to two years or fine for whoever negligently fails to recognise the insolvency or illiquidity, or acts with gross negligence in núms. 1, 2 or 7. Inc. 3: Art. 178 inc. 2 applies (cessation of payments or bankruptcy plus non-excludable link). No attempt clause. The record relies on inc. 1 núm. 6 letra a).

**Art. 180 (casos graves).** For Art. 178 inc. 1 conduct, prison up to ten years where the author acts to enrich himself or knowingly puts many people at risk of destitution or of losing assets entrusted to him ("con la intención de enriquecerse"). On the text as read, the cross-reference is to Art. 178, not Art. 179.

**Art. 181 (violación del deber de llevar libros de comercio)** (text of Ley 3440/08, in force from the day after publication, Aug 2008). Inc. 1, up to five years or fine: (1) failing to keep the commercial books the law requires, or keeping or altering them so the real position is hard to know ("omitiera llevar los libros de comercio"); (2) removing, hiding, destroying or damaging books or papers before the legal retention period ends; (3) contrary to law (a) drawing up balance sheets in a way that makes the real patrimonial position hard to know ("elaborara balances de tal manera que esto dificultare"), or (b) not drawing up the balance sheet or inventory in time. Inc. 2: negligent commission of núms. 1 and 3: up to one year or fine. Inc. 3: cross-reference to Art. 178. **CONFLICT:** the reading of Ley 3440 gave the cross-reference as "Art. 178 inc. 2" (the objective condition); the reading of the 1997 text gave "Art. 178 inc. 1 núm. 3". Check the source. If inc. 2 is right, Art. 181 also depends on cessation of payments or bankruptcy. The record relies on inc. 1 núm. 3 letra a).

**Art. 187 (estafa)** (1997 text). Inc. 1: whoever, intending an undue patrimonial benefit for himself or another, by a false statement about a fact causes an error in another that leads to a disposition of assets and a patrimonial loss: up to five years or fine ("mediante declaración falsa sobre un hecho"). Inc. 2: attempt is punishable ("será castigada también la tentativa"). Inc. 3: especially grave cases, up to eight years ("En los casos especialmente graves"). Inc. 4: Arts. 171 and 172 apply where pertinent (complaint requirements for family and trivial cases). The record charged inc. 1 and 2 (attempt); press says the appeals court recast it as consummated "estafa agravada" (inc. 3).

**Art. 192 (lesión de confianza)** (text of Ley 3440/08, in force 2009-07). Inc. 1: whoever, on the basis of a law, an administrative resolution or a contract, has assumed the duty to protect a relevant patrimonial interest of another and causes, or does not prevent, within the sphere of protection entrusted to him, a patrimonial loss: up to five years or fine ("será castigado con pena privativa de libertad de hasta cinco años"). Inc. 2: in especially grave cases the prison term may be raised to ten years ("podrá ser aumentada hasta diez años"); the aggravation does not apply below ten jornales. Inc. 3: applies even if the legal basis of the duty is invalid. Inc. 4: Arts. 171-172 apply. No attempt clause; no negligent variant. **Wording note:** the Ley 3440 reading returned "haya asumido la obligación de proteger"; the 1997 reading returned "haya asumido la responsabilidad de proteger un interés patrimonial". Verify which word the 2008 text uses. The record cites inc. 1 "modalidad causara".

**Art. 196 (lavado de activos)** (text of Ley 6452/19; the 1997 heading was "lavado de dinero", amended first by Ley 3440/08). Inc. 1: whoever converts or conceals an object stemming from an unlawful act, disguises its origin, or frustrates or endangers knowledge of its origin or location, its finding, its comiso, comiso especial or seizure: up to five years or fine ("convirtiera u ocultara un objeto proveniente de un hecho antijurídico"). Predicate unlawful acts: (1) those of CP Arts. 129a, 139, 184a, 184b, 184c, 185, 186, **187**, 188, 191a, 191b, **192**, 193, 200, 201, 246, 261, 262, 263, 268b, 268c, 300, 301, 302, 303 and 305; (2) any crimen; (3) an act by a member of a criminal association under Art. 239; (4) Ley 1340/88 Arts. 37-45; (5) Ley 4036/10 Arts. 94-104; (6) Ley 2422/04 Art. 336; (7) Ley 2523/04; (8) Ley 5810/17 Art. 227 e); (9) transnational bribery. **Arts. 179 and 181 are not on the list.** Inc. 2: same penalty for obtaining such an object and passing it on, or keeping or using it knowing its origin when obtained. Inc. 3: attempt punishable. Inc. 4: acting commercially or as member of a gang formed for continued laundering: up to ten years ("actuara comercialmente o como miembro de una banda"). Inc. 5: gross negligence as to origin: up to two years or fine. Inc. 6: inc. 2 not applied if a good-faith third party obtained the object earlier. Inc. 7: foreign predicates count if punishable where committed. Inc. 8: no punishment for voluntary reporting before discovery (plus helping secure the objects). Inc. 9: voluntary disclosure that considerably helps clarify the facts lets the court mitigate under Art. 67 or waive the penalty ("el tribunal podrá atenuar la pena con arreglo al artículo 67"). Inc. 10: autonomous offence ("no se requerirá sentencia sobre el hecho antijurídico subyacente"). Inc. 11: carve-out only for Art. 261 (tax evasion) predicates.

**Art. 239 (asociación criminal)** (1997 text as shown on BACN; I did not check for later amendment). Inc. 1, up to five years: whoever (1) creates an association structured hierarchically or otherwise organised to commit punishable acts ("creara una asociación estructurada jerárquicamente"); (2) is a member or takes part; (3) supports it economically or logistically; (4) renders services to it; (5) promotes it. Inc. 2: attempt punishable. Inc. 3: court may waive the penalty where reproach is slight or contribution secondary. Inc. 4: court may mitigate (Art. 67) or waive where the author voluntarily and diligently tries to prevent the association continuing or a planned act, or reports in time to the authority.

**"Administración en provecho propio".** Not in the Código Penal. It is Art. 8 of Ley 2523/04 (promulgated Dec 2004): "el funcionario público que decida, autorice o suscriba actos" or contracts that directly grant undue benefits for himself, spouse, partner or relatives to the second degree: prison up to ten years. Art. 2 scope: "toda persona que cumpla una función pública" or with power over public funds, services or property. Art. 7 (tráfico de influencias): up to three years or fine; five years where a judge or prosecutor is targeted. Source (primary): https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/4020/ley-n-2523-previene-tipifica-y-sanciona-el-enriquecimiento-ilicito-en-la-funcion-publica-y-el-trafico-de-influencias

### Inferences (mine, not from a source)

- All four offences in the record carry a base maximum of five years, so under Art. 13 they are delitos; that is why the attempt count needed the express clause in Art. 187 inc. 2, and why no attempt of Art. 192 or Art. 179 exists.
- The record's Art. 179 and (possibly) Art. 181 counts depend on the Art. 178 inc. 2 condition ("cesación de pagos" or declared "quiebra"). Whether a BCP resolution process under Ley 2334/03 satisfies that condition is a legal question the text does not answer. Ley 861/96 Art. 152 (below) says only the BCP may petition for a bank's bankruptcy, after liquidation.
- Laundering can attach to the estafa and lesión de confianza counts (both listed predicates) and, through núm. 3, to acts of a member of an Art. 239 association; it cannot rest on Arts. 179 or 181 alone.
- With several counts, Art. 70 would allow a single penalty above ten years (gravest frame ten years, raised by up to half). This is consistent with press talk of "up to 15 years", but that figure is the outlet's, not a court's.

### Gaps

- Arts. 171-172 text not read in full. Art. 57 and Art. 94 (referred to in Art. 196 inc. 4) not read.
- Whether Art. 239 or Art. 187 was amended after 2008 was not verified.
- I could not read the consolidated current Código Penal as a single text; the above combines the 1997 page with the two amending laws.

## 2. Código Procesal Penal (Ley 1286/98)

Source (primary): Corte Suprema de Justicia, Instituto de Investigaciones Jurídicas, "Cuarta edición digital actualizada. Año: 2024", amendments shown in footnotes. https://www.pj.gov.py/ebook/libros_files/codigo-procesal-penal.pdf

**Art. 19 (oportunidad).** With the court's consent the Ministerio Público may drop prosecution ("podrá prescindir de la persecución penal") where: (1) the act is trivial or the reproach minimal so that there is no public interest; (2) the Penal Code or other laws allow the court to dispense with penalty; (3) the expected penalty is insignificant next to one already imposed or expected elsewhere; (4) extradition or expulsion has been ordered. For (1) and (2) the accused must have repaired the damage, agreed reparation with the victim or shown willingness. May be requested until the preliminary hearing. Footnote: Art. 17 and Arts. 21-26 were touched by Ley 1444/99.

**Art. 75 (derechos del imputado).** "Al imputado se le asegurarán las garantías necesarias para su defensa". Rights listed: (1) no means contrary to dignity; (2) to be told the cause of arrest and who ordered it; (3) to name someone to be notified; (4) defender from the first act; (5) to present himself to the prosecutor or judge to be informed and heard on the facts ("presentarse al Ministerio Público o al juez"); (6) to abstain from declaring, and if he declares, to have his defender present; (7) no techniques that alter free will; (8) no restraints during acts. The record cites núm. 5.

**Art. 84 (libertad de declarar).** The accused may declare or stay silent and may declare as often as he wishes if pertinent and not dilatory. In the investigation he declares before the prosecutor in charge ("declarará ante el fiscal encargado de ella"); in the intermediate stage only if he asks, before the judge at the preliminary hearing; at trial as the Code provides. A statement is valid only with a defence lawyer present.

**Art. 85 (caso de aprehensión).** If deprived of liberty, the statement must be taken "a más tardar en el plazo de veinticuatro horas" from arrest, extendable by the same time if he asks to choose a defender; in exceptional cases the prosecutor may set another period by reasoned decision.

**Art. 86 (advertencias preliminares).** Before the statement the official must tell the accused the act attributed and a summary of the evidence, put the file at his disposal, and warn that he "podrá abstenerse de hacerlo y que esa decisión" cannot be used against him; and inform him of his procedural rights.

**Arts. 87-90.** Art. 87: order of the statement (identity data, then free account, then questions). Art. 88: no oath, no coercion ("ninguna clase de fuerza o coacción"). Art. 89: no leading or trick questions. Art. 90: police may not take an indagatoria.

**Art. 96 (valoración).** If the rules on the accused's statement are not observed, it cannot be used against him even with his consent; purely formal defects can be cured. Fragment: "La inobservancia de los preceptos relativos a la declaración".

**Art. 236 (proporcionalidad).** Deprivation of liberty during proceedings must be proportionate to the expected penalty ("deberá ser proporcional a la pena que se espera"); it may never exceed the statutory minimum penalty of the offence, nor the time limit for ending the proceedings, nor two years.

**Art. 242 (prisión preventiva).** After hearing the accused, the judge may order it "solo cuando sea indispensable" and only if all three concur: (1) sufficient evidence of a grave punishable act; (2) the accused's presence is needed and facts reasonably indicate authorship or participation; (3) facts sufficient to suppose risk of flight or obstruction of a specific investigative act.

**Art. 243 (peligro de fuga).** Factors: lack of roots ("la falta de arraigo en el país"); the penalty that may be imposed; the importance of the damage and the accused's attitude to it; conduct in this or earlier proceedings. Must be stated expressly in the order.

**Art. 244 (peligro de obstrucción).** Grave suspicion that the accused will destroy, alter, hide or falsify evidence, influence co-accused, witnesses or experts, or induce others to do so. "Estos motivos sólo podrán servir de fundamento" until the end of the trial.

**Art. 245 (medidas alternativas)** (footnote: amended by Ley 6350/2019). Where the risk can be avoided by a less burdensome measure "el juez, de oficio, deberá imponerle" one or more of: house arrest; supervision by a person or institution; periodic appearance; ban on leaving the country or an area; ban on attending places; ban on contacting persons; bail (a lawyer's personal bail not accepted); any other compatible measure. No economic bail where the accused cannot meet it. Unjustified breach makes the suspended detention enforceable. Gap: the fetch did not show any list of offences for which alternatives are barred; earlier versions of Art. 245 (Ley 4431/11) had such a bar and I could not confirm what Ley 6350/19 kept.

**Arts. 302-303 (imputación).** The prosecutor formalises the charge in an acta ("formulará la imputación en un acta") identifying the accused, describing the facts briefly and stating the time he needs; the judge then notifies and fixes the exact date for the accusation.

**Art. 350 (indagatoria previa).** "En ningún caso el Ministerio Público podrá formular acusación" unless the accused has had sufficient opportunity to give an indagatoria; for offences without prison, a written opportunity suffices.

**Art. 351.** Other conclusive acts: definitive or provisional dismissal; the prosecutor may also ask for conditional suspension, opportunity, abbreviated procedure or conciliation.

**Arts. 420-421 (procedimiento abreviado).** "Hasta la audiencia preliminar, se podrá proponer" it if: (1) the offence carries a maximum penalty below five years or a non-custodial sanction (as read by the tool); (2) the accused admits the act and consents; (3) the defender certifies free consent. Co-defendants do not block it for one. Art. 421: joint or separate written request; the judge hears the accused and the victim; "El juez podrá absolver o condenar, según corresponda."; a conviction cannot exceed the penalty asked by the accusers; if rejected, the admission cannot count as confession. **Note:** a 2023 press report (below, Banco Alemán) describes an abbreviated procedure in a lesión de confianza / estafa case, offences whose maximum is five years. Either the threshold wording differs from the tool's reading ("below five" vs "up to five") or practice differs; verify the text of núm. 1.

**Arts. 456-457.** The appeal court has competence "exclusivamente en cuanto a los puntos" challenged; where only the accused appealed, the decision "no podrá ser modificada en su perjuicio". These are the provisions defences invoke against the appeals ruling that added offences (see criminal_case.md).

### Cooperation / reduced penalties

- The CPP portions read contain no "arrepentido" or "colaborador eficaz" rule (primary, negative finding on Arts. 366-end; earlier parts were not searched exhaustively).
- Hoy (14 May 2023, secondary): the "delación premiada" figure "aún no existe en Paraguay"; the only similar tool is Ley 1340/88 Art. 43 (drug offences: penalty cut to one fifth if information is given before preventive detention, one third if later), which "solo tiene aplicabilidad en lo concerniente al combate al narcotráfico"; bills of 2020 and 2023 did not prosper as of that date. https://www.hoy.com.py/especiales/delacion-premiada-la-materia-que-aun-sigue-pendiente-en-paraguay
- What does exist in the texts read (primary): CP Art. 196 inc. 8 (no punishment for timely voluntary report) and inc. 9 (mitigation or waiver for disclosure that considerably clarifies); CP Art. 239 inc. 3-4 (waiver or mitigation); CP Art. 28 (desistance); CPP Art. 19 (opportunity); CPP Arts. 420-421 (abbreviated procedure); conditional suspension of proceedings (named in Art. 351; its own article, Art. 21, was not read).

### Gaps

- Whether any law after May 2023 created a general cooperation regime was not established.
- Art. 21 (suspensión condicional del procedimiento) and Arts. 136-137 (maximum duration of proceedings) not read.

## 3. Ley 861/96 General de Bancos, Financieras y Otras Entidades de Crédito, and Ley 5787/16

Sources (primary):
- Ley 861/96, BACN page (promulgation shown as 1996-06-24; file uploaded 2015; appears to be the unamended 1996 text). https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/4135/ley-n-861-general-de-bancos-financieras-y-otras-entidades-de-credito
- Ley 5787/16, BACN, promulgated 2016-12-19, published 2016-12-26. Art. 1 amends Arts. 3, 11, 12, 14, 21, 26, 35, 36, 37, 38, 43, 45, 47, 48, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 55, 56, 58, 71, 86, 89 and 91 of Ley 861/96. Art. 4 repeals Arts. 54 and 59 to 69 and Ley 4851/13. https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/8367/ley-n-5787-de-modernizacion-y-fortalecimiento-de-las-normas-que-regulan-el-funcionamiento-del-sistema-financiero-paraguayo

### Directors

- **Art. 34.** Management follows the law, the bylaws and BCP / Superintendencia rules.
- **Art. 35 (amended).** Board of a president and at least four directors; "deben ser personas físicas que reúnan condiciones de probidad, idoneidad" and experience.
- **Art. 36 (amended).** Incompatibilities for "presidentes, directores, gerentes o síndicos" and internal accountants / auditors.
- **Art. 37 (amended).** Changes in board composition must be notified to the Superintendencia (two business days in the 1996 text).
- **Art. 38 (amended, "Responsabilidad").** President and directors are "especialmente responsables" for: (a) approving operations and adopting agreements in breach of this law or other financial-system rules ("Aprobar operaciones y adoptar acuerdos con infracción"); (b) not implementing effective integral risk-management or corporate-governance policies, or not adopting measures to correct irregularities; (c) not complying with orders or information requests of the Superintendencia or BCP; (d) not giving timely and truthful information to the Superintendencia, or falsifying facts or operations that could affect stability and solvency; (e) not answering communications they must answer; (f) not ensuring timely external audit; (g) not complying with applicable laws and BCP / SIB rules. Final paragraph: "El Directorio del Banco Central del Paraguay sancionará las infracciones" according to gravity under Ley 489/95, without prejudice to civil or criminal actions. (1996 version: seven similar letters, "Omitir la adopción de las medidas necesarias" in letter b.)
- **Síndicos.** No dedicated article in the bank law portions read; they appear in Art. 36 (incompatibilities), Art. 70 e)-f) (insider lending) and Art. 118 (SIB may convene the assembly if board or síndico fail to). Their general duties come from the Código Civil (not retrieved; see section 8).

### Related parties ("vinculados") and risk units

- **Art. 46 (1996 text, not in the amended or repealed list).** Entities must identify and evaluate related persons and companies in the loan book as "una sola unidad de riesgo". Related companies: those in which the debtor holds enough shares to impose his will at the meeting, or with which he has any agreement or relation allowing him to impose it. Ownership is attributed through majority-held legal persons and through spouse and relatives to the fourth degree of consanguinity and second of affinity.
- **Art. 47 (amended 2016, "Otras personas vinculadas").** One risk unit is formed by persons who have among themselves relations of direction, management or control, "relaciones estables de negocios", or capital or administrative ties that allow one or more of them a dominant and continuous influence over the others' decisions. Also one risk unit where: a loan to one is presumed to benefit another; debtors have cross-guarantees on the same collateral; or one debtor's repayment capacity depends closely on another through relations hard to replace in the short term. New in 2016: the Superintendencia may, on objective and reasoned presumptions, identify other kinds of links; the BCP board regulates the article. (1996 wording: "ejercer influencia preponderante y continua sobre las decisiones".)
- **Art. 59 (1996, REPEALED by Ley 5787/16 Art. 4).** Total credit to persons linked to ownership or management "no puede exceder de un monto equivalente al 20%" of effective equity. Ownership-linked: holders, directly or indirectly, of more than 10% of capital. Management-linked: persons related to directors, managers and main officers. Terms could not be more favourable than market.
- **Art. 64 (1996, REPEALED).** Credit to one resident person could not exceed 20% of effective equity, 30% with collateral accepted by the SIB. Art. 63 (non-residents 5%, 20% with collateral), Art. 60 (other local financial entities 30%), Art. 61, 62, 65-69 also repealed.
- **Art. 58 (amended 2016, "límites prudenciales").** Limits on amounts, percentages, terms or ratios to protect solvency and system stability; the BCP "establecerá los límites prudenciales, conforme a la exposición de riesgos". The statute itself now sets no percentage for related parties or single borrowers.
- **Art. 71 (amended).** Entities breaching prudential limits "estarán sujetas a la inmovilización de fondos" at the BCP, as set by general resolution, without prejudice to other sanctions. (1996: daily fine on the excess at the average lending rate.)
- **Art. 70 (1996 text, not amended or repealed).** Banks may not: (a) lend against their own shares or those of other financial entities; (b) lend to finance purchase of such shares; (c) give guarantees of indeterminate amount or term; (d) pledge fixed assets (exceptions); (e) grant unsecured loans to "sus trabajadores, directores, administradores y síndicos" or guarantee them above 0.5% of effective equity per person and 10% in aggregate (1% and 20% with accepted collateral); (f) deal with directors, administrators, síndicos or their related entities on terms more favourable than ordinary clients; (g) buy shares of non-financial companies that own shares in the bank; (h) do operations not authorised.
- **Art. 72.** Sanctions in this law are without prejudice to those in the BCP organic law.

### Capital and solvency

- **Art. 11 (amended).** Minimum capital for banks: "G. 50.000.000.000 (Guaraníes cincuenta mil millones)", inflation-adjusted. Chambers 2025 (secondary) gives the updated figure as PYG 67,830,000,000 for banks.
- **Art. 43 (amended).** Effective equity = Tier 1 (capital principal: paid-in capital, irrevocable advances, etc.) plus Tier 2.
- **Art. 56 (amended).** "Capital regulatorio" means the solvency index. (a) Tier 1 to risk-weighted assets and contingents "no podrá ser inferior al 8% (ocho por ciento)". (b) Tier 1 plus Tier 2 "no podrá ser inferior al 12% (doce por ciento)" nor required above 14%. The BCP board, with SIB opinion, may raise the generic minimum gradually (at most 1 point a year) and impose additional risk capital of up to 3% of risk-weighted assets by systemic importance or risk profile. Ley 5787 Art. 2 fixes initial risk weights and the 8% / 12% minima; Art. 3 defines systemically important entities. (1996 text: single 8% ratio, BCP could raise to 12%.)

### Portfolio sales, secrecy, liquidation

- **Art. 40 núm. 9.** Banks may "Celebrar acuerdo de participación y de venta de cartera." Núm. 29 covers participation certificates in homogeneous portfolios. The statute sets no conditions on sales; those are in BCP rules (section 6).
- **Art. 86 (amended).** Bank secrecy does not apply to requests from the BCP and supervisors, a competent court, the Contraloría, tax and customs authorities, "la Fiscalía General del Estado y los agentes fiscales" within their powers, SEPRELAD, and between credit entities.
- **Arts. 118-121 (1996).** Intervention; board and management suspended; SIB runs the entity. Largely superseded for resolution by Ley 2334/03 (its Art. 36 replaces and repeals parts of Ley 861/96, per BACN page).
- **Art. 138 a).** The liquidator may bring any lawful action "contra los directores y trabajadores" to protect the entity, shareholders or creditors.
- **Art. 152.** Only the BCP, after liquidation, may ask for the entity's bankruptcy; the bankruptcy law then governs the "calificación de la conducta patrimonial".

### Inferences

- For a 2024-2026 fact pattern the 20% related-party cap of old Art. 59 is not a statutory rule; the applicable caps are whatever the BCP fixed under Art. 58. Until that resolution is read, any statement such as "related-party lending exceeded the legal limit of X%" cannot be anchored to a number from this file.
- Art. 47's "presumed to benefit another" and "dominant and continuous influence" tests are the statutory hooks for treating nominally separate borrowers as one unit or as related to the bank.
- Art. 38 a) and d) are the administrative-law mirror of the criminal allegations (approving operations in breach of rules; false information affecting solvency), and Art. 38 can supply the "law" source of the duty required by CP Art. 192, alongside the director's mandate.

### Gaps

- No consolidated current text of Ley 861/96 was found; the above overlays Ley 5787/16 on the 1996 page. Amendments after 2016 were not searched.
- Amended Art. 36 full list and Arts. 48-53 not paraphrased here.

## 4. Ley 2334/03 de Garantía de Depósitos y Resolución

Source (primary): BACN, promulgated and published 2003-12-12; page shows Arts. 1-37 and no amendment notes (uploaded 2014). https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/2513/ley-no-2334-garantia-de-depositos-y-resolucion-de-entidades-de-intermediacion-fi

- **Art. 1 (guarantee).** Cover per natural or legal person, per entity, "hasta el equivalente a setenta y cinco (75) salarios mínimos mensuales"; 100% for deposits securing foreign-trade operations and judicial bonds (letter g). The guarantee is first set off against the depositor's debts to the entity (letter e). Excluded (letter f): deposits of managers and directors and of persons linked to the bank under Arts. 46-47 of Ley 861/96 ("Quedará excluido de la garantía el conjunto de imposiciones"), and deposits that are "negocios simulados, indirectos, fraudulentos o ilegales". Chambers 2025 (secondary) puts 75 minimum wages at roughly USD 28,000.
- **Arts. 2-5 (FGD).** Fund created ("Se crea el Fondo de Garantía de Depósitos (FGD)"), administered and accounted for by the BCP but separate from its assets. Funding: one-off State contribution equivalent to USD 50 million; private contributions at "una tasa trimestral de contribuciones del 0,12%" of average deposit balances, stopping when the fund reaches 10% of system deposits; contingent State bonds. Fund assets cannot be attached.
- **Art. 6 (regularisation triggers).** Include a "déficit del índice de solvencia por debajo del límite legalmente exigible" lasting five business days; reserve shortfalls; breach of prudential limits for more than ten consecutive days; losses in two quarters threatening solvency; reliance on BCP emergency lending; repeated non-compliance; false reporting; abnormally high deposit rates.
- **Art. 7.** Entity must self-report within two business days; otherwise the SIB may fine the directors jointly 5% of paid-in capital. Plan decided within five business days; regularisation period no longer than 90 calendar days.
- **Art. 8.** SIB may require real or personal guarantees from shareholders and directors where the trigger is insufficient capital.
- **Art. 13 (causales de resolución).** (a) "haber suspendido el pago de sus obligaciones" (more than one obligation unpaid when due, attributable to the entity, without just cause); (b) "insuficiencia en el índice de solvencia" "por debajo del cincuenta por ciento (50%)" of the legally required minimum; (c) "el rechazo al Plan de Regularización por la Superintendencia de Bancos", or its early termination; (d) "la revocación de la autorización para operar" by the BCP board under Art. 94 inc. e) of Ley 489/95. The record states the Ueno resolution (BCP Resolución N° 1 of 2026-11-02) rested on inc. b).
- **Art. 14.** The BCP board must order resolution "dentro de las veinticuatro horas de haber tenido conocimiento" of a ground; the Superintendent appoints intervenors; liabilities-side operations suspended; publication.
- **Art. 15.** Intervenors "ocupará[n] físicamente todos los locales de la entidad afectada" and take the books.
- **Art. 16.** On publication, rights of administrators, shareholders and creditors are suspended and directors, managers and agents cease. Núm. 4: unauthorised acts of administration or disposal are void and must be returned, "sin perjuicio de las responsabilidades penales emergentes del hecho" for the officer and the beneficiary.
- **Art. 20 (order of payment).** First order, in sub-order: (1) guaranteed deposits of private parties and public social-security deposits; (2) cash mandates, tax collections and withholdings; (3) guaranteed deposits of public entities. Second order: credit obligations to the BCP. Third order: non-credit obligations to the BCP, FGD rights from its securitisation contributions, and the entity's tax debts. Second and third orders enter the exclusion balance only if securitisation is used.
- **Art. 25.** "Durante el procedimiento de resolución no podrán decretarse embargos."
- **Art. 26.** Privileged creditors not fully paid in resolution keep preference afterwards.
- **Art. 27.** BCP, SIB and intervenor officials cannot be sued for acts done in their legal functions; later judicial review for omission or deviation remains.
- **Art. 29.** President and administrators of systemic-risk funds answer civilly and criminally for their use.

### Inferences

- "Solvency below 50% of the minimum" under Art. 13 b) combined with Art. 56 of Ley 861/96 means an index below 6% (half of 12%) or Tier 1 below 4%; which of the two ratios the BCP applied is not stated in the sources read.
- Art. 1 f) makes related-party status directly relevant to depositors: deposits of persons linked under Arts. 46-47 lose the guarantee.

### Gaps

- **CONFLICT / check:** Art. 13 d) cites Art. 94 e) of Ley 489/95; on the 1995 text revocation sits in Art. 95 e), and after Ley 6104/18 the entity-sanction article is Art. 94. Not material to inc. b).
- No article requiring the BCP or SIB to report to the Ministerio Público was found in this law.
- Later amendments to Ley 2334/03 were not searched.

## 5. Ley 489/95 Orgánica del BCP (and Ley 6104/18)

Sources (primary): Ley 489/95, BACN (1995 text, no amendment notes): https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/699/ley-n-489-organica-del-banco-central-del-paraguay ; Ley 6104/18, promulgated 2018-07-03, published 2018-07-06: https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/8424/ley-n-6104-modifica-y-amplia-la-ley-n-48995-organica-del-banco-central-del-paraguay . Ley 6104 amends, among others, Arts. 7, 8, 14, 19, 29, 31, 32, 34, 82-84, 89-91, 93-97, 106-107 and adds new provisions (its own Arts. 2-12).

### Superintendencia de Bancos

- **Art. 30.** Technical body with functional, administrative and financial autonomy.
- **Art. 31 (amended).** "Corresponderá en exclusividad al Banco Central del Paraguay", through the SIB, to supervise compliance and to adopt oversight, vigilance and disciplinary measures over banks, financieras, other credit entities, exchange houses and others the board designates.
- **Art. 32 (amended).** Superintendent appointed by the Executive from a shortlist of the BCP board; five-year renewable term.
- **Art. 34 (1995 letters a-l; 17 numbered powers after 2018).** Inspect and supervise; permanent preventive oversight of capital, risk concentration, governance and liquidity; issue warnings, binding requirements and precautionary measures; set internal-control, accounting and valuation rules; **report any irregularity to the BCP board promptly with the remedial steps** (1995 letter g); give administrators written inspection results with deadlines. Added in 2018: order loan-loss provisions; scrutinise share transfers and require identification of the ultimate controller (núm. 13); consolidated supervision of local and foreign operations (núm. 15).

### Sanctions on entities and individuals (as amended in 2018)

- **Art. 84.** "Normas de ordenación y disciplina" include SIB and board resolutions.
- **Art. 89.** Nineteen categories of grave faults, including unauthorised operations, capital shortfalls, excessive risk concentration, false or incomplete reporting to the BCP or the Central de Información, obstructing supervision, insufficient provisions. (1995 Art. 90 letters a-l: e.g. own funds below 80% of the required solvency level; non-occasional breach of risk limits; insufficient provisions.)
- **Art. 90.** Minor faults ("Son faltas leves aquellas acciones u omisiones", 1995 Art. 91).
- **Art. 91.** Those holding management, oversight or audit posts can be sanctioned; exempt if they did not know and had no reasonable indications, or objected promptly and in a documented way.
- **Art. 93.** Entities answer for faults of employees and administrators.
- **Art. 94 (entities).** Grave: limitation of activities; ban on dividends or new offices up to two years; fine from 101 to 5,000 monthly minimum wages; suspension up to one year; revocation of authorisation. Minor: reprimand or fine up to 100 minimum wages. (1995: fine 100-1,000; suspension up to 60 days.)
- **Art. 95 (individuals).** Grave: written warning; fine up to 500 monthly minimum wages; removal and disqualification from management or oversight posts for three to ten years; cancellation of registration. Minor: verbal warning or fine up to 50 minimum wages. (1995 Art. 96: fine 10-50; disqualification 3-5 years.)
- **Arts. 96-97, 106-107.** Grading criteria; summary proceeding before a BCP lawyer acting as instructing judge; provisional suspension of administrators by four board votes; appeals. Sanctioning power is independent of criminal proceedings (1995 Art. 84).
- **Prescription (1995 Art. 93).** Grave faults five years; minor one year.

### Liability and protection of BCP / SIB officials

- **Art. 16 (1995).** BCP directors are personally and jointly liable where board resolutions breach the law, unless their dissent is recorded in the minutes.
- **Arts. 12-15, 29.** Exclusivity, disqualifications, incompatibilities and recusal for BCP directors; officials may not be directors, shareholders, employees or advisers of supervised entities. Art. 14 (amended) bars persons with commercial, economic or professional ties that create conflicts of interest.
- **Art. 6 / Art. 7 (amended).** Secrecy duty of current and former officials, with criminal and other liability for breach; exceptions for judicial orders and others.
- **Ley 6104/18 new Arts. 4-9.** Administrative liability of officials for breach of the law or staff statute "without prejudice" to civil and criminal liability; minor and grave faults (grave include breach of professional secrecy, accepting improper benefits); sanctions up to dismissal with disqualification from public office for two to five years.
- **Ley 2334/03 Art. 27** (see section 4) is the only immunity-type rule found: no suits for acts in the exercise of legal functions in regularisation / resolution, subject to later judicial review.
- No express duty to report crimes to the Ministerio Público was found in either text (negative finding on the portions read). A general duty of public officials to report offences may sit in CPP Art. 286; that article was not read.

### Gaps

- Full text of amended Art. 34 (17 powers) and Art. 89 (19 grave faults) not itemised.
- Art. 19 (board powers) not read.

- **Corporate governance: Resolución N° 16, Acta N° 4 of 2022-01-20** (Chambers 2025, secondary): directors must be qualified, give sufficient time, exercise independent judgment, manage conflicts of interest; a director may hold executive posts in at most three entities. Also cited there: Resolución N° 8, Acta N° 51 of 2021-10-28 (share transfers, 5% threshold); Resolución N° 24, Acta N° 75 of 2010-11-11 (licensing).

### Gaps (important)

- Current numeric limits for related-party and single-borrower exposure; the text of Res. 15/24; the provisioning percentages by category under Res. 1/2007 as updated. These need the BCP site or a request to the BCP.

## 7. Ley 1034/83 del Comerciante (commercial books)

Source (primary): BACN, promulgated 1983-12-06, published 1983-12-16. https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/2538/ley-n-1034-del-comerciante

- **Art. 11 c)-d).** Merchants must keep orderly accounts and "conservar los libros de contabilidad, la correspondencia y los documentos" for the Art. 85 period.
- **Art. 74.** Merchants with capital above 1,000 minimum daily wages must keep regular accounts showing their patrimonial position and results.
- **Art. 75.** Free choice of system, "debiendo llevar indispensablemente un libro Diario y uno de Inventario".
- **Art. 77.** A registered accountant must be used; merchant and accountant are jointly responsible for faithful entries ("siendo ambos responsables solidariamente de que en los asientos se registren").
- **Art. 78.** Books presented to the Registro Público de Comercio to be numbered and stamped before use.
- **Art. 79.** Chronological, no gaps; "No podrán hacerse enmiendas, raspaduras ni cualquier otra alteración"; corrections by counter-entry; no removal of pages.
- **Art. 80.** The Diario records daily operations in order with creditor and debtor.
- **Art. 82.** The Inventario records opening position and year-end position with profit and loss.
- **Art. 83.** Annual balance sheet "dentro de los tres primeros meses de cada año".
- **Art. 85.** Books and records "deberán ser conservados por cinco años" from the last entry; vouchers for the same time from their date.
- **Arts. 86-87.** Companies keep these books plus those of their type; sociedades por acciones also share and bond registers, attendance and minute books.
- **Art. 89.** Dividends only from real profits in a properly drawn and approved balance sheet.
- **Art. 92.** Approval of the balance sheet "no implica la liberación de los administradores" or síndicos.
- **Arts. 95-104.** Exhibition and evidentiary value of books (entries prove against the keeper).

Inference: Arts. 74-85 are the "ley" that CP Art. 181 and Art. 179 núm. 6 refer to when they say books or balance sheets must be kept or drawn "contrary to law"; for banks, SIB accounting rules under Ley 489/95 Art. 34 add to them.

## 8. Código Civil (Ley 1183/85)

### Simulación, Arts. 305-310 (primary)

Source: BACN, Libro Segundo. https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/5299/ley-n-1183-codigo-civil-ii-parte-libro-segundo

- **Art. 305.** "La simulación no es reprobada por la ley" when it harms no one and has no unlawful purpose.
- **Art. 306.** "Se podrá anular el acto jurídico" when the simulation harms a third party or pursues an unlawful end; between the parties only under unjust-enrichment rules.
- **Art. 307.** "Si hubiere un contradocumento firmado por alguna de las partes", judges may weigh it and the simulation, if no legal prohibition or third-party right is infringed.
- **Art. 308.** "Los terceros perjudicados por un acto simulado tienen acción" to annul it; good-faith onerous acts with others stand.
- **Art. 309.** "La simulación no podrá ser opuesta por los contratantes" to good-faith creditors of the apparent owner; creditors of the simulating seller may attack the act and rank ahead of the apparent buyer's unsecured creditors if their claim is earlier.
- **Art. 310.** "La prueba de la simulación será admisible sin limitación" when a third party sues or when the aim is to show the act was unlawful.

### Director liability in sociedades anónimas, Arts. 1111-1115 (NOT retrieved as statutory text)

The BACN page for the relevant book (https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/5293/codigo-civil) redirected to a non-HTTPS address that the fetch tool would not follow. What follows is **secondary**: Sebastián Balbín, "El directorio de las sociedades anónimas en el derecho paraguayo" (teaching paper, MADE-UCA, Asunción, 2014), hosted by the Poder Judicial: https://www.pj.gov.py/ebook/monografias/extranjero/civil/Sebastian-Balbin-Apuntes-para-Directorio-MADE-UCA-Asunción-2014-2.pdf . The article numbers are Balbín's attributions and were not checked against the Code.

- **Art. 1111 (per Balbín).** Directors answer without limit and jointly to the company, shareholders and third parties for non-performance or bad performance of the mandate, for breach of law or bylaws, and for damage caused by dolo, abuse of powers or gross fault. Exempt is the director who did not take part in the resolution, or who recorded written dissent and informed the síndicos before liability was claimed. Balbín reads this as fault-based, with a rebuttable presumption of collective fault. Fragment he quotes (citing Arts. 99 and 1111): "los directores y administradores son responsables respecto a la persona jurídica".
- **Art. 1112 (per Balbín).** Approval of management, waiver or settlement by the assembly extinguishes liability to the company, unless the liability arises from breach of law or bylaws or shareholders holding one fifth of capital oppose.
- **Art. 1113.** Social liability action, decided by the assembly; a judgment of liability removes the director.
- **Art. 1114.** Shareholders may sue if the company does not (one-fifth minority; any shareholder after three months of board inaction).
- **Art. 1115.** In bankruptcy the trustee sues; directors answer to creditors for failing to preserve the company's assets.
- Pablo Costantini Avalos, "La sociedad anónima en el derecho paraguayo" (pj.gov.py monograph, undated, secondary) describes the standard as that of a diligent administrator acting loyally, and síndico duties (overseeing management and accounts, reporting to the assembly) under Arts. 1117 ff.; its article mapping is internally inconsistent, so do not cite numbers from it. https://www.pj.gov.py/ebook/monografias/nacional/civil/Pablo-Costantini-La-Sociedad-Anonima-Derecho-Paraguayo.pdf

Gap: statutory text of Arts. 1111-1112 and of the síndico articles (about Arts. 1117-1126) must still be pulled from BACN or the CSJ Código Civil e-book.

## 9. Ley 6446/19 (beneficial-owner registers)

Source (primary): BACN, promulgated 2019-11-29, published 2019-12-02; in force the day after publication (Art. 16). https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9116/ley-n-6446--crea-el-registro-administrativo-de-personas-y-estructuras-jurdicas-y-de-beneficiarios-finales-del-paraguay

- **Arts. 1, 14.** Creates the Registro Administrativo de Personas y Estructuras Jurídicas and the Registro Administrativo de Beneficiarios Finales, under the Ministerio de Hacienda (a Dirección General is created for them).
- **Art. 2.** Covers companies, associations, foundations and other legal persons, plus trusts and investment funds.
- **Art. 4 (beneficiario final).** A natural person who directly or indirectly holds a substantial participation or final control, or benefits, and meets at least one of: (a) holds 10% or more of capital; (b) "Controle más del 25% (veinticinco por ciento) del derecho de votación."; (c) is a manager or administrator, or habitually uses or benefits from the entity's assets or transactions; (d) can appoint or remove part of the management or supervisory bodies; (e) has control under the bylaws or other instruments.
- **Arts. 3, 5-7.** Existing entities: nine months from entry into force, per the authority's calendar; new ones: 45 business days from incorporation; changes: 15 business days. Indirect holdings require the chain of ownership, percentages, supporting documents and a sworn statement. Art. 6 lists the data per beneficial owner (identity, tax number, domicile, nationality, basis and date of the status).
- **Art. 8.** Until compliance: no new accounts, debt or equity instruments, deposits or remittances with financial-system entities; RUC blocked; other procedures suspended. A "constancia de cumplimiento" is issued on compliance.
- **Art. 9.** Fine of 50 to 500 jornales mínimos, or up to 30% of profits or dividends distributed; also for refusing or giving false, wrong or incomplete information.
- **Arts. 10-11.** The register is not public. Online access for the BCP, the tax authority (SET) and SEPRELAD; other authorities, prosecutors and courts may be given consultation; access by obliged entities of Ley 1015/97 as SEPRELAD regulates. A list of compliant entities is published at least every six months.
- **Art. 12.** Records kept five years.
- Secondary: Ferrere note on the later requirement that obliged entities ask for the registration certificate (not read): https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/exigencia-de-que-sujetos-obligados-de-la-ley-1-015-97-requieran-la-constancia-de-inscripcion-en-el-registro-de-beneficiarios-fin/

Inference: the 10%-of-capital test in Art. 4 a) matches the old 10% ownership test of repealed Art. 59 of Ley 861/96; letters (c)-(e) capture control without shareholding, which is the kind of link the record argues for group companies. Regulatory decrees and any amendment after 2019 were not checked.

## 10. Case law and doctrine on lesión de confianza by bank directors (all SECONDARY unless marked)

- **Banco Nacional de Trabajadores (BNT) "vaciamiento".** Poder Judicial press note, 2010-12-30 (official court communication; treat as reliable secondary): the Supreme Court's Sala Penal confirmed Acuerdo y Sentencia N° 37 of 2009-06-04 of the Tribunal de Apelación en lo Criminal, Primera Sala, in a case over losses of about Gs. 120,000,000,000. 22 of 23 defendants had been convicted; former bank presidents and most directors received ten years, some businesspeople and union leaders seven, two union leaders four. The note cites CP Art. 192 and says the Court found prescription had not run: "el punto neurálgico es el plazo razonable". https://www.pj.gov.py/notas/4794-confirman-condenas-en-caso-por-vaciamiento-del-bnt . Significance: ten-year terms show the Art. 192 inc. 2 "especially grave" frame applied to bank directors and to outside beneficiaries.
- **Banco Paraguayo Oriental (BPO).** Última Hora, 2007-07-05: directors and BCP officials were tried for lesión de confianza and asociación criminal; prosecutors said directors lent to relatives and friends, with losses of about Gs. 135,000 million. The Sala Penal (2-1) confirmed extinction of the action; "ya se operó la prescripción". https://www.ultimahora.com/corte-confirma-extincion-la-causa-del-bpo-n48396 . Significance: related-party lending by directors was charged as Art. 192, supervisors were co-accused, and delay ended the case.
- **Banco Alemán.** Última Hora, 2023-10-26: a former banker, extradited, was accused on 2 Oct 2023 of lesión de confianza, conducta indebida en situaciones de crisis and estafa as co-author over alleged diversion of funds; judge accepted an abbreviated procedure with the prosecution's agreement; two years suspended plus a Gs. 250 million donation. https://www.ultimahora.com/ex-banquero-jose-peirano-no-ira-a-la-carcel-y-donara-g-250-millones-por-caso-banco-aleman . Significance: the same three offences as in the Ueno record have been charged together before; outcome was negotiated two decades after the facts. ABC (2023-10-22) covers the related mutual-funds accusation (not read): https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2023/10/22/acusacion-en-caso-fondos-mutuos-banaleman-revela-ardid-para-desviar-inversiones/
- **1995 crisis (Bancopar, Banco General, Bancosur).** Última Hora retrospective, 2018-11-08: interventions from April 1995; causes named include liberalisation without adequate control, corruption and disregard of rules on related-party loans; "Se diezmó el sistema regulado". It reports no convictions. https://www.ultimahora.com/la-crisis-financiera-1995-n2777774
- **Doctrine.** Eduardo Royg Acha, "Consideraciones sobre la lesión de confianza en la administración empresarial" (pj.gov.py monograph, undated, after 2014): analyses Art. 192 inc. 1 in its active form for company administrators. Protected interest is patrimony; the duty arises from law, administrative resolution or contract, normally the contract for administrators; loss must be relevant and fall "dentro del ámbito de protección que le fue confiado"; only principal functions count; intent (including dolo eventual) is required, negligence and attempt are excluded. Cites Código Civil Art. 974 ("Los administradores son solidariamente responsables ante la sociedad"). It cites no Paraguayan judgments and does not discuss banks, business risk or shareholder consent. https://www.pj.gov.py/ebook/monografias/nacional/penal/Eduardo-Royg-Acha-Consideraciones-LC-AdmEmp.pdf
- **Judgments not read but located (primary if read):** the Poder Judicial "observatorio" hosts lesión de confianza rulings, e.g. "C.E.S.M. y otros s/ lesión de confianza y otros N° 6523/2009" https://www.pj.gov.py/images/contenido/dgagj/observatorio/C-E-S-M-Y-OTROS-SOBRE-LESION-DE-CONFIANZA-Y-OTROS-N-6523-2009.pdf and "G.C.Z. s/ lesión de confianza N° 108/2014" https://pj.gov.py/images/contenido/dgagj/observatorio/GCZ-SOBRE-LESION-DE-CONFIANZA-N-108-2014.pdf . Their subject matter is unknown to me.

### Gaps

- No reported judgment text on Banco Unión or Bancopar directors was found; I found no reliable source on how those prosecutions ended.
- No Paraguayan decision was found that states a test for when related-party lending by a bank director is a "perjuicio patrimonial" under Art. 192 (as opposed to a regulatory breach). The BNT judgments (first instance and A. y S. N° 37/2009) would be the place to look.
