Research cut-off: 20 November 2026. Units: "Gs. X millones" = X million guaraníes (so Gs. 1.000 millones = "mil millones" = one billion guaraníes in English short scale). Method caveat for the writer: rating-report figures below were machine-extracted from the PDFs; totals reconcile internally where I could check, but table cells should be re-verified against the PDF before publication. ## 1. For each company: what it sells, to whom, main brands, year founded, scale indicators

### Takeaway
Public sources give a real business description for the rated group companies (Grupo Vázquez, ITTI, Terravaz, Ueno Bank and its financial affiliates) and for a handful of borrowers (Pagopar, Hendyla, Tuiu, Franede/Alula Hotel, Technoma, Nubicom, Office Design, GSR, Sagas). For most of the June 2026 borrowers (SATEC, HD Invest, La Tropa Fletes, Factopar) and for Kasas Propiedades, Model, Galadriel, Henrici, Dataforge and Pear, no public description of an operating business was found: they appear in the press only as borrowers or conduits.

### Cited Findings

**Group operating companies**

- **Grupo Vázquez S.A.E.** — Holding with "more than 75 years" of history; 28 companies in six business units (financial services, technology, retail/entertainment, agribusiness, real estate, marketing/advertising, the last created in 2025); more than 6.000 direct employees (rated-report description, Dec 2025). — [S&R-GV May26](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)
- Grupo Vázquez self-description on its website: more than 20 companies, more than 5.500 employees, "daily revenue above US$1 million"; history dated from a 1945 workshop; Credicentro founded 1984 and renamed ueno Holding in 2022. (Company self-description, undated page.) — [grupovazquez.com Nuestra historia](https://www.grupovazquez.com/nuestra-historia/)
- FIX gives "more than 5.000" group employees and "more than 500" at Ueno Bank. — [FIX-GV Jul26](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)
- **ITTI S.A.E.C.A.** — Founded 2004 as IT Consultores (the group's site dates its acquisition and merger with J. Fleischman to 2021). Sells hardware, software and technology services to the financial sector, companies and Grupo Vázquez entities. Products/brands: Bankitti (core banking, formerly ITGF), FINERA, KADIKO, MilNegocio, itti secure. Business units: digital environment; fintech and digital banking; digital-experience services; security and multibiometrics; AI. More than 1.100 corporate clients; 3.184 staff of 16 nationalities (Dec 2025). — [S&R-ITTI May26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf); [grupovazquez.com](https://www.grupovazquez.com/nuestra-historia/)
- ITTI's Bankitti core "processes over 40% of Paraguay's financial transactions" per FIX; a press profile adds that nine regulated entities use it and lists Itaú, Sudameris, Continental, Tigo and the BCP as clients (the latter is company claim relayed by press). — [FIX-GV Jul26](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf); [Revista Mercado, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistamercado.do/money-invest/dueno-ueno-bank-miguel-vazquez-empresas/)
- **Red Digital S.A. (REDISA) / Wepa** — Payments-and-collections network and non-bank correspondent ("Sucursal Nativa": loan instalments, deposits/withdrawals, utility bills) and Western Union master agent in Paraguay; FIX says Wepa handles remittances and PIX integration and "manages UENO's non-branch network". — [itti.digital Red Digital page](https://www.itti.digital/red-digital/); [FIX-GV Jul26](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf); [S&R-Ueno Dic25](https://www.ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Informe-de-Calificacion-UENO-BANK-Dic25-firmado_Solventa.pdf)
- Scale of the network the bank relied on (bank-level, not REDISA-specific): no physical branches; former Visión Banco branches converted into non-bank correspondents and self-service points; 25.000+ non-bank correspondents, 926 self-service terminals, 103 card dispensers, 75 experience centres; more than 2,7 million bank clients (Dec 2025). — [S&R-Ueno Dic25](https://www.ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Informe-de-Calificacion-UENO-BANK-Dic25-firmado_Solventa.pdf)
- An Infonegocios headline refers to Wepa as a Western Union digital channel with "más de 1.500 puntos de recepción de remesas" (headline only; article not opened, date not verified). — [Infonegocios](https://infonegocios.com.py/y-ademas/wepa-canal-digital-de-western-union-funcionara-24-horas-mas-de-1-500-puntos-de-recepcion-de-remesas)
- **Red Digital de Procesamiento S.A. (REDIPRO) / UPAY** — Payment processor with Visa and Mastercard acquirer licences (FIX); described by Solventa as Ueno's "own payment processor". Products include uPos terminals; in Aug 2025 it launched acceptance of Argentine interoperable QR wallets (sponsored content, no merchant or volume figures). — [FIX-GV Jul26](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf); [La Nación py (patrocinado), 27 Aug 2025](https://www.lanacion.com.py/patrocinado/2025/08/27/upay-rompe-barreras-y-lidera-la-interoperabilidad-de-pagos-en-paraguay/)
- Brand mapping conflict: Solventa pairs Red Digital = Wepa and Red Digital de Procesamiento = Upay; one ABC article writes "Redisa (Upay)" and "Redipro (Wepa)". — [S&R-GV May26](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf); contradicted by [ABC 12-Oct](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/12/ueno-movio-us-146-millones-hacia-tres-empresas-vinculadas/)
- **Vinanzas S.A.** — Purchase, management and recovery of credit portfolios (collections); launched 2020 as a group "digital solution"; ITTI held 49,73% at Dec 2025; Solventa's bank report calls it an outsourced ("tercerizada") collections manager and says the bank's collections team has 130+ professionals. — [Parámetro 4-Nov](https://elparametro.com/la-ruta-del-dinero-de-ueno-prestamos-a-empresas-transferencias-entre-cuentas-y-millones-que-terminaban-en-su); [grupovazquez.com](https://www.grupovazquez.com/nuestra-historia/); [S&R-ITTI May26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf); [S&R-Ueno Dic25](https://www.ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Informe-de-Calificacion-UENO-BANK-Dic25-firmado_Solventa.pdf)
- **Kaitel** — Kaitel SRL is the Argentine branch (opened Nov 2024) of a Paraguayan company of the same name, set up to "provide human-resources outsourcing services developing technology"; its Argentine staff did software maintenance, data analysis and development for Ueno Bank; the Paraguayan parent was reportedly transferred in 2025 to WOM 77 SA and Impresión, Distribución y Logística SA (both ITTI investees). 800–900 Argentine workers were suspended without pay after the bank's closure. — [Chequeado, 11 Nov 2026](https://chequeado.com/el-explicador/del-ueno-bank-a-buenos-aires-asi-operaba-el-grupo-vazquez-con-sus-trabajadores-en-argentina/)
- **Ueno Casa de Bolsa S.A.** — Brokerage; distributes the group's mutual funds; on 11 Nov 2026 the Superintendencia de Valores ordered client portfolios transferred to other brokers. A 29 Oct 2026 headline reports its mutual funds kept an AA rating (headline only). — [5Días especial Ueno](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno); [La Nación py, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios_edicion_impresa/2026/10/29/fondos-mutuos-de-ueno-casa-de-bolsa-mantienen-calificacion-aa/)
- **Ueno Seguros S.A.** — Insurer, formerly Alfa Seguros (joined the group 2022, renamed 2023); after the bank's resolution it said it "continues operating normally"; no intervention ordered by the insurance superintendency as of 5 Nov 2026. Holds 3,03% of ITTI. — [100% Seguro, 5 Nov 2026](https://100seguro.com.ar/la-crisis-de-ueno-bank-abre-interrogantes-sobre-el-futuro-de-ueno-seguros-y-las-empresas-del-holding/); [S&R-ITTI May26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf)
- **Ueno AFPISA** (Administradora de Fondos Patrimoniales de Inversión) — Manages two mutual funds; about US$64,6 millones under management at end-June 2026, of which US$6,74 millones (10,4%) was placed in Ueno Bank; "thousands" of investors; funds suspended as of 11 Nov 2026 and the securities regulator was seeking a replacement manager on 16 Nov 2026. — [5Días, 12 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/article/ueno-afpisa-tena-el-104-de-fondos-mutuos-en-ueno-bank-y-el-resto-en-grandes-bancos); [5Días especial Ueno](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)
- A later press piece puts the mutual-fund investments at "more than USD 40 million" (no date for the figure). — [1000 Noticias, 19 Nov 2026](https://1000noticias.com.py/2026/11/19/inversores-de-ueno-casa-de-bolsa-reclaman-informacion-sobre-mas-de-usd-40-millones-en-fondos-mutuos/)
- **Terravaz S.A.E.** — Real-estate developer/manager; incorporated 28 Aug 2018 as Radice, renamed Terravaz in 2025. Main asset: Fideicomiso Shopping Costanera (Encarnación): shopping centre of about 10.000 m² leasable area, 54 tenants, 93% occupancy; Executive Center (65 offices occupied, 7 vacant); 228 parking spaces. The shopping centre produced 97,94% of 2025 revenue. 48 employees. — [S&R-Terravaz Jun26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Informe-de-Calificacion-TERRAVAZ-PEG-USD3-Dic2025.pdf)
- **Kasas Propiedades S.A.E.** — No public business description found. Appears as an issuer/seller of bonds bought by Factopar and La Tropa Fletes (USD 10.049.628,97) and as a debtor of Ueno Bank. — [UH 7-Nov](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)
- **Model S.A.** — No public business description found. Appears as seller of bonds to La Tropa Fletes (USD 2.117.596,26) and as a debtor of Ueno Bank. — [UH 7-Nov](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)
- **Technoma S.A.E.C.A.** — Sells computer/communications equipment, IT infrastructure, software licences, maintenance and IT security, largely to the State; operating since May 2010; described by El Prisma as a Grupo Vázquez company. DNCP data cited: 157 procurement processes for six group companies in 2023–2026 totalling G. 287.630 millones, of which Technoma 87% = G. 250.151 millones (DNIT G. 88.777 millones; BCP G. 59.618 millones; Ministry of Economy G. 31.582 millones). — [El Prisma, 13 Nov 2026](https://elprisma.com.py/technoma-la-empresa-del-grupo-vazquez-que-acaparo-contratos-con-el-estado/)
- **Discover Paraguay** — ITTI investee (94,63% at Dec 2025); no description found; listed with G. 10.147 millones of obligations at Ueno. — [S&R-ITTI May26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf); [UH 7-Nov](https://www.ultimahora.com/grandes-deudores-de-ueno-aparecen-en-la-investigacion-del-bcp-sobre-desvio-de-fondos)
- **MUV** — Brand of Click S.A. (ITTI 60%); "technology services platform" added to the group in 2023. No user or revenue figures found. — [S&R-ITTI May26](https://syr.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Informe-de-Calificacion-PEG-G1-ITTI-Dic25.pdf); [grupovazquez.com](https://www.grupovazquez.com/nuestra-historia/)
- **Estancia Don Miguel / cattle business** — Division of Grupo Vázquez S.A.E. itself. Don Miguel (Puerto Pinasco): 16.000 ha (6.500+ ha natural reserve, 9.000 ha pasture), capacity 7.000 breeding cows, 3.000+ head sold in 2025; Costa Flor (Itacurubí del Rosario): 620 ha silvopastoral; biological assets Gs. 20.128 millones at Dec 2025. — [S&R-GV May26](https://www.bolsadevalores.com.py/uploads/2026/05/GRUPO-VAZQUEZ-PEG-G1-y-USD1-Mayo26.pdf)
- FIX version: 17.000 ha, 8.500 ha pasture, about 6.500 cattle (target 15.000), second establishment 620 ha with about 600 head, forestry 12.000–15.000 tonnes of wood; agribusiness about 25% of Grupo Vázquez's (individual) revenue. — [FIX-GV Jul26](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)
