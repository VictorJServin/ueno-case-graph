## 2. FRANEDE S.A. (Frandede): hotels and projects, transfer from Grupo Vázquez, Ueno loans

### Takeaway
Franede is the legal entity behind Alula Hotel in Shopping Costanera, Encarnación, and is reported as borrower for hotel projects in Asunción (Hotel Chaco; a hotel on calle Palma in the Bar Nacional building). ABC Color reports it belonged to Grupo Vázquez until 30 June 2025, when its shares passed to Mauro Vargas, and that Ueno lent it G. 314,001 million in total, with funds allegedly moved on to Vinanzas S.A. and Itti SAECA the same day.

## 4. The case: status, offences, measures, defence

### Takeaway
He is one of the 27 charged on 4 Nov 2026 (no. 23 in Última Hora's list), in the group of 19 people from associated companies. As of 20 Nov 2026 he is not detained and has no personal precautionary measure; his hearing for imposition of measures is set for 18 Dec 2026, with prosecutors seeking a travel ban and bail. A general ban on selling or encumbering assets already applies. No statement by him or a lawyer was found.
