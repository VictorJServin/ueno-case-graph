# Cadiem (CADIEM Casa de Bolsa S.A. / CADIEM AFPISA) and the Ueno Bank case — reporting to 20 Nov 2026

Scope note: all facts below are from press reports published 30 Jul – 17 Nov 2026. Cadiem's own sites (cadiem.com.py, cadiemfondos.com.py) blocked automated fetching, and one MarketData page failed to load, so primary documents (fund fact sheets, SIV resolutions, rating reports on Cadiem) were NOT read directly. Where an article summary could be wrong on detail, it is flagged.

Bottom line for the writer: Cadiem appears in Ueno coverage as an **affected investor/fund manager** (its mutual funds held Ueno-linked instruments, were briefly suspended by the regulator, and suffered a run of redemption requests). In the reporting found, Cadiem is **not** named by prosecutors, is **not** among the companies or 27 accused in the criminal case, and **no** corporate, shareholder or family link to Grupo Vázquez/Ueno is reported. No raid, complaint to prosecutors or sanction against Cadiem was found.

## 1. What is Cadiem: business lines, size, ownership, executives, regulator, ratings

### Takeaway
Cadiem is a 23-year-old Paraguayan brokerage plus fund administrator, reported as the largest mutual-fund manager by share (18.6% of industry AUM at mid-2026), with roughly US$400-440 million in funds, about US$1.46 billion in total client assets, and 20,000-22,000 clients. Ownership is not disclosed in the press found; on 17 Nov 2026 Argentina's Balanz Capital announced a memorandum to buy the majority stake.

## 2. Exposure of Cadiem's funds or clients to Ueno Bank and Grupo Vázquez issuers; suspensions and redemption limits after 3 Nov 2026

### Takeaway
Cadiem's mutual funds held instruments of Ueno Bank and of two unnamed companies of its economic group; one figure reported is 11.73% of one (unnamed) fund's equity as of 3 Nov 2026. The SIV suspended redemptions and subscriptions in five Cadiem funds, ordered Ueno Bank instruments valued at zero, lifted the suspension on 6 Nov, and Cadiem then paid redemptions in stages after requests reached about 40% of fund assets (about US$160 million).

### Cited Findings
- 3 Nov 2026: Cadiem's first statement said it was following the BCP's resolution procedure for Ueno Bank, evaluating effects, and handling a high volume of client queries; no exposure figures given — [ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-mensaje-de-cadiem-a-sus-inversionistas-tras-el-anuncio-del-bcp/)
- Five Cadiem funds had redemptions and new subscriptions temporarily suspended under SIV Resolución SV.SG. N.º 0015/2026: Disponible Renta Fija en Guaraníes, Disponible Dólares Americanos, Proyección Guaraníes, Proyección Dólares, Crecimiento Renta Fija en Guaraníes. Cadiem said this was not a closure or liquidation — [Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)
- Cadiem told investors some funds hold instruments issued by Ueno Bank and by two companies of its economic group (not named), with exposure varying by fund; no amounts given. It stressed fund investments are not deposits at Ueno Bank — [Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)
- Valuation: the SIV suspended trading in Ueno Bank instruments and set extraordinary criteria valuing them at zero during the resolution; other criteria were under review for the related companies. Cadiem said it could not yet say how much might be recovered. Resolutions cited: SV.SG. N.º 0014, 0015, 0016/2026 and Circular SV.SG. N.º 020/2026 — [Revista PLUS, 5 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/05/crisis-de-ueno-cadiem-explica-que-pasa-con-sus-fondos-mutuos/)
- Investments linked to the Ueno Bank economic group were 11.73% of "the fund's" equity per the portfolio at 3 Nov 2026; the fund is not named; expected annual return quoted at 8.00% — [Última Hora, c. 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/panico-financiero-bonos-soberanos-caen-y-casa-de-bolsa-sufre-demanda-del-40)
- Redemption requests reached about 40% of the roughly US$400 million under management, about US$160 million; Cadiem said meeting them at once would force sales in a volatile market — [Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/cadiem-enfrenta-pedidos-de-rescate-por-us-160-millones-tras-la-crisis-de-ueno/); [La Nación, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/cadiem-arma-un-plan-de-contingencia-tras-solicitudes-de-rescate-por-el-40-de-sus-fondos/)
- An unnamed Cadiem director: "Lo que ha ocurrido el martes ha generado un pánico general entre nuestros inversores"; directors Gelay, Ayala Person and Paredes appeared in a video statement — [Última Hora, c. 6 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/panico-financiero-bonos-soberanos-caen-y-casa-de-bolsa-sufre-demanda-del-40)
- 6 Nov 2026: the SIV lifted the suspension for funds of Cadiem AFPISA and Puente AFPISA; funds of BASA AFPISA and BNB Asset Management AFPISA had already resumed; only Ueno AFPISA's two funds stayed suspended. Administrators had to apply the new valuation criteria before resuming — [Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/bcp-levanta-restricciones-a-fondos-de-cadiem-y-puente-pero-mantiene-las-de-ueno/); [Diario HOY, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/06/ueno-sigue-sin-habilitar-retiros-de-fondos-mutuos-bcp-explica-que-pasa-con-el-dinero-de-los-inversores)
- Staged payouts: earlier requests paid 6-16 Nov; requests of 9-13 Nov paid 17-23 Nov; later requests had no confirmed date. Cadiem's 6 Nov statement set a 5% limit for holders of more than 20 units; investors said the worked example gave about 15% and asked for a correction. Amounts used the 5 Nov 2026 unit value. Cadiem said the SIV approved the schedule, that some requesters cancelled and new money came in, that it had no compensation proposal, that the BCP provides no money, and that the deposit guarantee fund does not cover mutual funds. It promised consolidated data on 12 Nov — [ABC Color, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/11/ueno-bank-que-piden-los-inversores-de-casa-de-bolsa-afectados-y-cuando-cobraran-los-retiros/)
- Investor group (spokesperson Marcelo Tolosa; "more than 500" investors per ABC, "345" per 5Días) asked Cadiem for per-fund exposure, the investment limits applied, the share of unit-value change due to Ueno-linked holdings, waiver of early-withdrawal charges, and that Cadiem cover part of losses with its own money. They also asked about units in Fondo Mutuo Crecimiento tied to assets separated from Frigorífico Concepción and Cidesa (non-Ueno issuers) — [ABC Color, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/11/ueno-bank-que-piden-los-inversores-de-casa-de-bolsa-afectados-y-cuando-cobraran-los-retiros/); [5Días, 10 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/article/cadiem-cuestiona-a-la-superintendencia-y-alerta-sobre-la-prdida-de-confianza)
- Context on group bonds in the market: about US$181 million of capital-market bonds outstanding at Dec 2025 (US$124 million Grupo Vázquez, US$41 million ITTI, rest Terravaz); Ueno Bank issued a US$350 million bond in March 2026 — [FIX SCR rating report on Grupo Vázquez S.A.E., 2 Jul 2026](https://www.grupovazquez.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/Grupo-Vazquez-S.A.E._compressed.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "two companies of the economic group" are plausibly among the listed bond issuers (Grupo Vázquez S.A.E., ITTI, Terravaz), but no article names them; do not state it as fact.
- The 11.73% figure applies to a single fund; it should not be applied to the whole US$400 million.

## 5. How other brokerages and fund managers were affected (comparison)

### Takeaway
The hardest hit were group-affiliated or defaulting intermediaries: Ueno Casa de Bolsa and Valores Casa de Bolsa were suspended by the BVA for repo settlement failures, and Ueno AFPISA's two funds stayed frozen. Cadiem, Puente, BASA and BNB funds were suspended only briefly and reopened by 6 Nov.

### Cited Findings
- On 5 Nov 2026 the BVA preventively suspended Ueno Casa de Bolsa S.A. (note 222/26; default 3 Nov; firm said upcoming repo maturities would not be settled "hasta nuevo aviso") and Valores Casa de Bolsa S.A. (note 224/26; repo settlement failures on 29 Oct, 2 Nov and 4 Nov) for lacking funds to complete repo repurchases; amounts not stated; no end date — [ABC Color, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/06/ueno-bank-bva-suspende-a-ueno-y-valores-casa-de-bolsa-por-falta-de-fondos-para-cumplir-operaciones/)
- The BVA said it kept operating normally and that the two suspensions stemmed from "incumplimientos particulares" in repo settlement — [Revista PLUS, 11 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/11/bolsa-de-valores-aclara-su-rol-en-la-crisis-de-ueno-y-deslinda-responsabilidades/)
- Fund managers with Ueno-linked funds temporarily suspended: Cadiem AFPISA, Puente AFPISA, BASA AFPISA, BNB Asset Management AFPISA (all resumed by 6 Nov) and Ueno AFPISA (two funds still suspended for operational impediments) — [Revista PLUS, 6 Nov 2026](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/06/bcp-levanta-restricciones-a-fondos-de-cadiem-y-puente-pero-mantiene-las-de-ueno/)
- Investors in Ueno Casa de Bolsa's mutual funds complained on 10 Nov of nearly a week without answers and asked for transfer to another administrator — [ABC Color, 10 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/10/ueno-bank-inversionistas-afectados-reclaman-informacion-al-bcp-sobre-sus-fondos-y-plazos-de-solucion/)
- About 30,000 investors in Ueno-linked mutual funds were awaiting a solution; representatives met the SIV to stop a liquidation and seek transfer to another administrator — [Diario HOY, 15 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/15/unos-30000-inversionistas-de-fondos-mutuos-en-ueno-aguardan-una-solucion)
- Headlines seen but not read: Cavapy to transfer Ueno Casa de Bolsa client accounts (ABC Color, 11 Nov 2026); BVA suspended trading of all Ueno Bank bonds (Revista PLUS / MarketData, 3 Nov 2026) — [ABC Color](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/11/cavapy-trasladara-las-cuentas-de-ueno-casa-de-bolsa-que-deben-hacer-los-clientes/); [Revista PLUS](https://revistaplus.com.py/2026/11/03/bolsa-de-asuncion-suspende-negociacion-de-todos-los-bonos-de-ueno-bank/)

### Inferences
- Cadiem's problem was liquidity and valuation from holding Ueno-linked paper, a different category from the settlement defaults at Ueno and Valores Casa de Bolsa.
