# Ueno Bank S.A. collapse: investors, creditors and the international dimension (public reporting to 20 Nov 2026)

Scope note: all wrongdoing described below is allegation as reported by the cited outlet. Dates are publication dates unless stated. "Fetched" means the page text was read; "headline only" means only a search-result title was seen and the content is unverified. Baseline timeline used throughout: the BCP board decided on 2 Nov 2026 to open the resolution procedure for ueno bank S.A. and announced it on 3 Nov 2026 (Portfolio.hu 3 Nov 2026; Perfil 5 Nov 2026).

## 1. OTP Bank (Hungary): stake, amounts, vehicle, statements, write-down

### Takeaway
OTP's investment is confirmed by multiple sources: US$30m in December 2025 plus US$17m in June 2026 (US$47m total) for 9.99% of U Holdings, the Luxembourg parent of the bank, with under 1% of the votes according to Paperjam. After the collapse OTP said it was a purely financial investor with no control rights, booked a US$32m negative fair-value adjustment in its Q3 2026 report, and its deputy CEO said it learned of the problems too late; no source found names "U Luxemburgo" as an entity, and no source reports conditions set by OTP or a further planned increase.

### Cited Findings
- 23 Dec 2025: OTP Bank PLC reported as becoming a strategic shareholder of ueno bank with a US$30m investment, described as completing a US$85m capitalization plan due to conclude by end-2025; ueno described as a subsidiary of "U Holdings SARL", a Luxembourg holding; stake size not stated; article credits La Tribuna — [RDN, 23 Dec 2025](https://rdn.com.py/2025/12/23/otp-bank-expands-into-paraguay-through-ueno-bank-investment/)
- 3 Jun 2026: ueno's own release says OTP's total strategic investment in U Holdings (parent of ueno bank) is US$47m and its ownership "will rise to 9.99%", making it the company's "main shareholder"; no conditions, approvals or future increases mentioned — [ueno.com.py, 3 Jun 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/?p=13476)
- 3 Jun 2026: same figures (US$47m, 9.99% of U Holdings, "socio principal"); ueno president Juan Manuel Gustale quoted calling OTP a long-term shareholder and strategic partner and "una clara señal de confianza"; no OTP executive quoted — [La Nación (Py), 3 Jun 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/06/03/banco-europeo-se-vuelve-socio-principal-de-ueno-bank-con-inversion-de-usd-47-millones/); [La Política Online, 3 Jun 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/empresas-py/ueno-suma-al-banco-mas-grande-de-hungria-como-su-socio-principal/)
- Structure per Luxembourg filings as reported by Paperjam: U Holdings incorporated 29 Sep 2023 by Grupo Vázquez at 9 Rue de Bitbourg, Luxembourg City; it holds 100% of U Paraguay SA (the company set to control the bank), carried at US$482.4m after a Dec 2025 contribution, against U Paraguay equity of US$225.3m; OTP's US$47m gives 9.99% of U Holdings but less than 1% of voting rights; Grupo Vázquez subscribed five million profit-sharing shares for US$50,000 carrying one vote each, and holds about 86.73% of economic capital and 98.82% of votes — [Paperjam (Luxembourg), undated page viewed 20 Nov 2026](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/ueno-bank-the-paraguayan-bankruptcy-that-leads-to-luxembourg)
- Paperjam also reports earlier in-kind contributions of U Paraguay shares valued at US$40.7m (Sep 2023) and US$181.9m (about Mar 2024) against much lower published U Paraguay equity (US$4.3m; US$31.6m at end-2024), with no impairment recorded by the board; and that on 20 Mar 2024 shares were distributed to 22 new shareholders including group executives and government-linked figures — [Paperjam](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/ueno-bank-the-paraguayan-bankruptcy-that-leads-to-luxembourg)
- 29 Oct 2026: ueno announced a US$100m recapitalisation, half from U Holdings, subject to approvals (no OTP commitment specified) — [Paperjam](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/ueno-bank-the-paraguayan-bankruptcy-that-leads-to-luxembourg)
- 3 Nov 2026: Hungarian press reports the collapse of "OTP's Paraguayan investment": US$47m for a stake of nearly 10% in the parent — [Portfolio.hu, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.portfolio.hu/befektetes/20261103/bedolt-az-otp-paraguayi-befektetese-tobb-millio-ugyfel-penze-valt-elerhetetlenne-873966)
- 3 Nov 2026: OTP told 444/Telex it was only a financial investor with no control or management rights, that this was not a bank acquisition, that the investment fits its conservative risk policy, is a fraction of a percent of equity above US$18bn and about 1.5% of annual profit above US$3bn — [Telex, 3 Nov 2026](https://telex.hu/gazdasag/2026/11/03/bedolt-ueno-bank-paraguay-otp-bank-befektetes); [Index.hu, 3 Nov 2026](https://index.hu/gazdasag/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-paraguay-bedolt-otp-befektetes/) (Index converts US$47m to about HUF 15.32bn)
- 5 Nov 2026: 444 repeats the two-step purchase, US$47m, nearly 10%, and OTP's "financial investor only" line — [444.hu, 5 Nov 2026](https://444.hu/2026/11/05/bocsanatot-kert-majd-lemondott-paraguay-elnoke-az-otp-altal-is-finanszirozott-ueno-bank-osszeomlasa-utan)
- 11 Nov 2026: OTP's Q3 2026 report contains a US$32m negative fair-value adjustment on the stake; deputy CEO László Bencsik said "we did not know about the problems, and the regulator did not know either", that due diligence was done but less thorough than for a full acquisition, called the matter "very bad and unpleasant", said the loss is not significant for OTP, and compared it to Wirecard (2020); OTP earlier put the stake at about 0.2% of its market cap — [Világgazdaság (vg.hu), 11 Nov 2026](https://www.vg.hu/penz-es-tokepiac/2026/11/otp-bank-a-holtterbol-ugrott-elo-a-paraguayi-bankcsod)
- 14 Nov 2026: Bloomberg reports OTP "now records a US$32 million charge" and quotes Bencsik speaking after results; it states OTP raised its stake to nearly 10% "in August" — [Bloomberg Línea, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/escandalo-de-ueno-bank-pone-en-duda-la-reputacion-de-paraguay-como-destino-de-inversion/)
- 19 Nov 2026: OTP, in a written statement to EFE, defended the investment and cited problems "que no se podían prever"; said Ueno's attraction was its commercial success and neobank model outside Europe; said exposure is not material versus 2025 profit of about US$3.25bn and assets of about US$138bn; Bencsik (to HVG) said OTP "no tenía conocimiento de los problemas"; EFE restates 9.99% via U Holdings, which is 86.73% controlled by Grupo Vázquez — [EFE via Unitel, 19 Nov 2026](https://unitel.bo/noticias/agencias/el-otp-bank-hungaro-defiende-haber-invertido-en-el-ueno-y-habla-de-problemas-imprevisibles-KO24916590)

### Inferences
- The "main shareholder / socio principal" wording in ueno's June 2026 release conflicts with the Luxembourg filings described by Paperjam and EFE (Grupo Vázquez 86.73% economic, 98.82% votes); OTP was at most the largest outside minority holder.
- US$32m charge on a US$47m outlay implies about US$15m was still carried at end-Q3 reporting; no source says whether the remainder will be written off.
- The names in the brief map as follows: "U Paraguay" = U Paraguay SA (intermediate company under U Holdings); "U Luxemburgo" does not appear in any source found and is most likely a colloquial label for U Holdings (Luxembourg).

### Gaps
- Timing conflict: second tranche dated June 2026 by ueno, La Nación, vg.hu and EFE; Bloomberg says the stake reached nearly 10% "in August". Possibly announcement vs closing; unresolved.
- No source found for any conditions OTP attached, any option or plan to go above 9.99% in 2026, board seats, or legal action by OTP.
- OTP's own IR release / Budapest Stock Exchange announcement and the Q3 report were not retrieved directly; figures above are via press. A GuruFocus earnings-call summary exists but could not be fetched.
- Paperjam article carries no visible publication date.
- EFE's OTP shareholder breakdown (foreign 54.45%, local 39.41%, MOL 8.57%, Groupama 5.1%) sums to over 100% as relayed; do not reuse without checking.

## 2. Other foreign investors and lenders (DFIs, bondholders, underwriters)

### Takeaway
Confirmed foreign creditors are IDB Invest (guaraní loan of about US$20m, Nov 2024), Triodos Bank and GAWA Capital (US$17.5m, Aug 2026), and holders of the US$350m 6.70% five-year 144A/Reg S notes issued in March 2026 (bookrunners BofA Securities and J.P. Morgan per La Política Online), which fell from about 86 cents to about 5 cents. No post-collapse statement by any development lender was found.

### Cited Findings
- 18 Nov 2024: IDB Invest announced a local-currency loan of G. 150,000m (about US$20m) to ueno bank in two tranches of G. 75,000m, funded by IDB Invest bond issuance on the Paraguayan market (CADIEM Casa de Bolsa as broker), for MSME lending, with technical assistance — [IDB Invest, 18 Nov 2024](https://www.idbinvest.org/en/news-media/idb-invest-and-ueno-bank-join-forces-promote-financial-inclusion-paraguay)
- 6 Aug 2026: ueno announced US$17.5m of new long-term funding: Triodos Bank (Netherlands) US$11.75m, four years; GAWA Capital (Spain) US$5.8m, three years; for SME and green finance — [ueno.com.py, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/?p=15302)
- 3 Mar 2026 closing (announced 9 Mar 2026): inaugural international bond, US$350m senior notes, 6.70% coupon, five years, Rule 144A/Reg S, New York law, demand 1.5x; rated by Fitch and S&P — [ueno.com.py, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.ueno.com.py/en/2026/03/09/ueno-bank-us-350-million-senior-notes-the-largest-ever-by-a-paraguayan-bank/)
- 21 Oct 2026: the 2031 notes had fallen to about 87 cents from a 99-cent debut; director Alejandro Gómez Abente promised a capital announcement before end-October; government deposits at Ueno were about G. 5 trillion (US$869m), 29% of deposits at end-June 2026 — [Bloomberg Línea, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/banco-paraguayo-ueno-busca-reforzar-su-capital-en-medio-de-caida-de-bonos-y-escrutinio-regulatorio/)
- 14 Nov 2026: Ueno's international bonds began November above 85 cents and fell to about 5 cents; Alpha Credit Advisors' César Fernández said Paraguayan corporates are effectively shut out of global markets — [Bloomberg Línea, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/escandalo-de-ueno-bank-pone-en-duda-la-reputacion-de-paraguay-como-destino-de-inversion/)
- 9 Nov 2026 (single-source, partisan-leaning political outlet; treat with caution): bookrunners named as BofA Securities and JP Morgan; holders named with nominal amounts: Western Asset Management about US$10m, Nomura about US$1.4m across two funds (trimmed April–July), VanEck US$600,000, Optimum US$510,000, "among others"; indicative quotes 5–10 cents on 6 Nov; international funds said to be preparing action under New York law; article also asserts that JP Morgan, the SEC, the FBI and the US DOJ were looking at how the bonds were sold — [La Política Online, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/economia-py/los-fondos-internacionales-preparan-una-demanda-por-los-bonos-basura-de-ueno-bank/)
- 3 Nov 2026: the BCP's Superintendencia de Valores (Res. SV.SG. 0014/2026; BVA Circular 13/2026) suspended public offering and trading of all securities issued by ueno bank, including financial and subordinated bonds originally issued by Visión Banco, because resolution suspends interest and amortization payments — [La Nación (Py), 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/suspenden-la-negociacion-de-todos-los-bonos-emitidos-por-ueno-bank/)
- Domestic institutional creditors for context: about US$850m of public funds at Ueno as of June incl. state pension money (Reuters, 11 Nov 2026); IPS about US$355m (Infobae 5 Nov 2026) vs about US$516m (EFE 15 Nov 2026); CONMEBOL G. 85,470m (about US$14.3m) in savings certificates frozen (EFE 11 Nov 2026) — [Reuters via WSAU, 11 Nov 2026](https://wsau.com/2026/11/11/fitch-places-paraguays-rating-on-watch-for-possible-downgrade-after-ueno-collapse/); [EFE via Infobae, 15 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/11/15/lo-que-se-sabe-y-lo-que-viene-despues-de-la-intervencion-de-ueno-bank-en-paraguay/); [EFE via Primicias, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.primicias.ec/deportes/futbol/crisis-financiera-paraguay-ueno-bank-deporte-bancos-136872/)

### Inferences
- Senior noteholders and DFI lenders are unsecured creditors of a bank in resolution; a 5-cent price implies the market expects near-total loss, consistent with the BCP's stated plan to use about US$600m of "healthy assets" first for depositors and pension funds (EFE, 15 Nov 2026).
- The US-agency involvement claimed by La Política Online is not corroborated by any wire or US primary source found.

### Gaps
- IFC, BIO, Proparco, DEG, CAF: no evidence found of exposure to Ueno. FMO: a project page (fmo.nl/project-detail/67140) surfaced in a Ueno/IDB search but was not opened; exposure unconfirmed.
- No post-collapse statement found from IDB Invest, Triodos or GAWA; outstanding balances unknown.
- Correspondent banks: nothing found.
- Bookrunner names confirmed only by La Política Online (ueno's release shows them in an image only). Bondholder list is from one outlet; no filing reviewed.
- No bondholder lawsuit confirmed as filed by 20 Nov 2026.

## 3. Credit ratings before the collapse and downgrades after

### Takeaway
Until mid-October 2026 Ueno carried BB (S&P, Fitch) internationally and AA-py (FIX SCR) / AApy (Solventa&Riskmétrica) locally; Fitch moved the outlook to negative on 16 Oct 2026. FIX cut the bank to Dpy and Grupo Vázquez S.A.E. and ITTI S.A.E. from A+py to Cpy in reports dated 3 Nov 2026, saying information had been withheld; S&P and Fitch are reported to have gone to D, though exact dates/primary releases were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Dec 2024: S&P research update "Ueno Bank S.A. Rated 'BB'; Outlook Stable" (20 Dec 2024) and Fitch "Rates UENO Bank's IDR 'BB'; Outlook Stable" are posted on ueno's site (titles only seen) — [S&P PDF on ueno.com.py](https://www.ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/RatingsDirect_ResearchUpdate_UenoBankS.A.RatedBB_OutlookStable_3302759_Dec-20-2024.pdf); [Fitch PDF on ueno.com.py](https://ueno.com.py/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Fitch-Rates-UENO-Banks-IDR-BB_-Outlook-Stable.pdf)
- Dec 2025: ueno described as the only Paraguayan institution with dual international ratings (S&P, Fitch) plus local ratings from FIX and Solventa&Riskmétrica — [RDN, 23 Dec 2025](https://rdn.com.py/2025/12/23/otp-bank-expands-into-paraguay-through-ueno-bank-investment/)
- 16 Oct 2026: Fitch affirmed long-term IDRs at 'BB', VR 'bb', short-term 'B', raised the Government Support Rating to 'bb-' from 'b+', and revised the outlook to negative from stable, citing potential capital erosion from rapid balance-sheet growth and high intangibles (Fitch Core Capital 10.3% at end-Q2 2026 after deducting intangibles; 15.2% at end-2025 without the deduction), a loan-to-deposit ratio up to 91.9%, growing reliance on wholesale funding, and heightened scrutiny of related-party transactions, public-sector fund concentration and accounting; reported NPL 0.6%; D-SIB status noted — [Última Hora, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/de-estable-a-negativa-esto-es-lo-que-dice-fitch-en-su-calificacion-a-ueno)
- 5 Nov 2026: pre-collapse local ratings were Solventa&Riskmétrica AApy/stable (communicated by the bank 27 Apr 2026) and FIX SCR AA-py/"fuerte"; FIX cut to Dpy on 3 Nov 2026 saying it received relevant information the bank had not provided (undisclosed related-party operations, asset-quality problems); Solventa&Riskmétrica also assigned Dpy (date not given) — [Palermo Online, 5 Nov 2026](https://palermonline.com.ar/wordpress/quienes-calificaron-a-ueno-las-buenas-notas-antes-del-derrumbe-de-paraguay/) (secondary outlet)
- FIX follow-ups: on 5 Nov 2026 FIX said the intervention uncovered undisclosed information; on 10 Nov 2026 it said certain transactions had been concealed or incorrectly accounted for — [Big4News, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.big4news.com/p/pwc-ueno-bank-collapse-audit-accounting)
- Group issuers: FIX report dated 3 Nov 2026 cut Grupo Vázquez S.A.E. from A+py to Cpy and seven bond programmes from pyA+ to pyC, citing ties to the bank (bank = 23% of group assets and 59% of EBITDA at Dec 2025; group indirect stake above 90%); group debt ex-bank US$341m incl. US$204m of notes vs about US$71m cash (June 2026); next interest payments from 19 Nov 2026; further cut to Dpy/pyD if a payment is missed — [La Nación (Py), 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/crisis-de-ueno-bank-golpea-al-grupo-vazquez-calificadora-rebaja-su-nota-y-advierte-alto-riesgo-de-incumplimiento/)
- ITTI S.A.E.: FIX cut from A+py to Cpy and five bond programmes from pyA+ to pyC (report 3 Nov 2026); 59% of ITTI revenue comes from the bank; total debt US$66m incl. US$40m notes — [La Nación (Py), 6 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/06/caso-ueno-itti-cae-de-a-a-c-en-calificacion-de-riesgo-y-advierten-posible-incumplimiento-de-deudas/)
- S&P: downgraded Ueno Bank S.A. to 'D' from 'BB' on 4 Nov 2026 — [Wikipedia, "2026 Paraguay banking crisis"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Paraguay_banking_crisis) (tertiary; primary release not seen)
- Fitch: issuer cut to 'D' and the notes to 'C' with poor recovery expectations (date not given) — [La Política Online, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/paraguay/economia-py/los-fondos-internacionales-preparan-una-demanda-por-los-bonos-basura-de-ueno-bank/)
- Moody's did not rate Ueno in any source found; its commentary is on the system and sovereign (see section 5).

### Inferences
- Ueno held non-investment-grade international ratings and high-but-not-top local ratings; no agency flagged insolvency risk before 16 Oct 2026, and FIX has publicly attributed its miss to information withheld by the issuer.

### Gaps
- Feller Rate and Moody's Local: no rating of Ueno or group issuers found.
- Primary Fitch and S&P downgrade releases not retrieved; dates rest on Wikipedia (S&P, 4 Nov) and an undated mention (Fitch). A Traders Union headline "Fitch cuts ratings to D" could not be fetched.
- Date of the Solventa&Riskmétrica downgrade and any rationale not found.
- A FIX report dated 3 Nov 2026 is hosted on bcp.gov.py (seen in search results only; not opened).

## 4. Assets found or frozen abroad

### Takeaway
As of 13 Nov 2026 prosecutors said several countries had confirmed assets linked to Ueno and related companies, but they did not name the countries and no foreign freeze has been confirmed; the United States, Luxembourg and Argentina are named only as recipients of informal inquiries.

### Cited Findings
- 9 Nov 2026: prosecutors announced informal inquiries with the United States, Luxembourg and Argentina to trace and recover Ueno-linked funds — [AFP via El Observador CR, 13 Nov 2026](https://observador.cr/varios-paises-hallan-activos-vinculados-a-escandalo-de-ueno-bank-en-paraguay-fiscalia-acusa-a-presidente-pena/)
- 12 Nov 2026: prosecutor Francisco Cabrera held a preliminary meeting with Luxembourg prosecutors on a possible cooperation request — [AFP via El Observador CR, 13 Nov 2026](https://observador.cr/varios-paises-hallan-activos-vinculados-a-escandalo-de-ueno-bank-en-paraguay-fiscalia-acusa-a-presidente-pena/)
- 13 Nov 2026: prosecutors said several countries (unnamed) confirmed the presence of assets and that freeze requests were being prepared; more than US$800m of public funds remained unaccounted for per the article; the same day prosecutors sought to add a criminal-association charge against former president Santiago Peña (who denies the charges) — [AFP via El Observador CR, 13 Nov 2026](https://observador.cr/varios-paises-hallan-activos-vinculados-a-escandalo-de-ueno-bank-en-paraguay-fiscalia-acusa-a-presidente-pena/); [Al Jazeera with AFP/Reuters, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/paraguays-ex-president-pena-faces-new-charge-over-ueno-bank-collapse)
- Luxembourg angle: Paraguayan prosecutors contacted Luxembourg authorities to locate accounts and assets with a view to seizure and repatriation; no Luxembourg action beyond receipt is reported; no diverted amount in Luxembourg has been publicly established; share registers, contribution deeds and some payment proofs are held there — [Paperjam](https://en.paperjam.lu/article/ueno-bank-the-paraguayan-bankruptcy-that-leads-to-luxembourg)
- Domestic measures for contrast: freezes on corporate and personal accounts of executives reported at US$70m (attributed to Última Hora, 3 Nov 2026) and seizures of vehicles, aircraft and a yacht in raids (8 Nov 2026) — [Wikipedia, "2026 Paraguay banking crisis"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Paraguay_banking_crisis) (tertiary)
- Chile: a headline reports the Ueno brand was registered in Chile and an executive hired there (headline only, not opened) — [Roadshow.cl](https://roadshow.cl/ueno-bank-de-paraguay-los-nexos-con-chile/)

### Inferences
- The accurate formulation is "located, freeze requested/being prepared", not "frozen abroad". Luxembourg is the most concrete foreign nexus because the holding and its corporate records sit there.

### Gaps
- Which countries confirmed assets, what the assets are, their value, and which foreign authorities responded: not disclosed in any source found.
- No confirmation from US, Luxembourg or Argentine authorities found.

## 5. Spillover: banks, deposits, guaraní, sovereign bonds, BVA; IMF, agencies, foreign governments

### Takeaway
Market reaction was sharp but contained: sovereign spread about 119 to 144 bp (peak 157) by 5 Nov, guaraní down roughly 12% in a week, long sovereign bonds down 2–3% by 13 Nov, two brokerages suspended at the BVA and Ueno mutual funds frozen. Moody's (6 Nov) saw no immediate sovereign impact (Baa3 stable); Fitch put BB+ on Rating Watch Negative on 11 Nov; no IMF statement on the crisis was found.

### Cited Findings
- System weight: Ueno held 8% of system loans and 9% of deposits at Sept 2026; about 97% of depositors estimated fully covered; deposit guarantee fund liquidity US$1.6bn (about twice Ueno's insured deposits) — [Expansión citing Moody's, 6 Nov 2026](https://expansion.mx/economia/2026/11/06/quiebra-de-ueno-bank-exhibe-fallas-de-supervision-y-corrupcion-en-paraguay-advierte-moodys)
- About 69% of Ueno deposits were outside guarantee coverage in July; public deposits about 29%; a state guarantee of public deposits could cost about US$830m (1.3% of GDP) — [Bloomberg Línea Brasil, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/internacional/do-ueno-bank-a-renuncia-de-pena-como-o-paraguai-perdeu-a-fama-de-porto-seguro-regional/)
- Other banks: Asoban called the system sound and liquid and Ueno an "caso particular aislado" — [Infobae, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/11/05/impacto-financiero-por-el-colapso-del-ueno-bank-salto-el-riesgo-pais-de-paraguay-y-el-guarani-se-devaluo-8/). System total capital ratio 17.3% vs 12% minimum per IMF Article IV (28 Aug 2026) — [Rio Times, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.riotimesonline.com/paraguay-bond-risk-ueno-bank-imf-us-brazil)
- BCP liquidity step: temporary 5-point cut in reserve requirements, with freed funds restricted to buying CDAs or financial bonds; planned tender of Ueno assets/liabilities in lots to other institutions; about US$600m of "healthy assets" identified by BCP president Jorge Schreiner — [EFE via Infobae, 15 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/11/15/lo-que-se-sabe-y-lo-que-viene-despues-de-la-intervencion-de-ueno-bank-en-paraguay/)
- Guaraní: dollar at G. 5,661 on 28 Oct vs 6,358 on 5 Nov (+12.3%); +5.4% on 5 Nov alone — [Infobae, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/11/05/impacto-financiero-por-el-colapso-del-ueno-bank-salto-el-riesgo-pais-de-paraguay-y-el-guarani-se-devaluo-8/); wholesale 6,358.5, retail G. 6,720 at one bank — [Perfil, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.perfil.com/noticias/amp/economia/crisis-politica-en-paraguay-subio-el-riesgo-pais-y-el-guarani-se-devaluo-un-5-a46yz0p74JD4.phtml). IIF's Jonathan Fortun said the correction began before Ueno (Brazil elections, soy seasonality) — [Bloomberg Línea Brasil, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/internacional/do-ueno-bank-a-renuncia-de-pena-como-o-paraguai-perdeu-a-fama-de-porto-seguro-regional/)
- Sovereign risk: JP Morgan country-risk index 119 to 144 bp over five to six sessions, touching 157 on 5 Nov; 2036 bond yield 6.04%, highest since June 2025; JP Morgan underweight on Paraguay sovereign credit (attributed to ABC Color) — [Perfil, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.perfil.com/noticias/amp/economia/crisis-politica-en-paraguay-subio-el-riesgo-pais-y-el-guarani-se-devaluo-un-5-a46yz0p74JD4.phtml)
- Sovereign bond prices 2–13 Nov (MEF data): 2050 85.64 to 83.08 (-2.99%; yield 6.65% to 6.90%); 2048 -2.74%; 2044 -2.57% (6.57% to 6.83%); 2036 -1.99%; 2031 -0.85%; article notes global conditions also played a part — [ABC Color, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/14/bonos-soberanos-de-paraguay-profundizan-su-caida-luego-de-la-crisis-de-ueno-bank/)

## 6. Sports and entertainment sponsorships

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- APF: contract Aug 2023–Aug 2027 worth US$12m; Olimpia: bank helped finance land purchase next to the stadium and has a sponsorship tied to the stadium name; Cerro Porteño: bank bought the club's debt to Visión Banco and signed stadium naming rights through 2030; Comité Olímpico Paraguayo: US$300,000 through June 2027 (already paid, per COP president Camilo Pérez); Rally del Paraguay: three-year deal through 2027, US$1m direct sponsorship, organisers' CEO César Marsal said they cannot continue with the sponsor — [InfoNegocios Paraguay, 4 Nov 2026](https://infonegocios.com.py/default/que-compromisos-estan-en-juego-y-quienes-podrian-verse-afectados)
