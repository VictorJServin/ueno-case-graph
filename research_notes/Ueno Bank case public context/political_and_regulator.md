# Ueno Bank collapse: political and regulatory-accountability dimension (reporting up to 20 Nov 2026)

Researcher notes on method and reliability (read first):
- All facts below carry outlet + publication date. Everything about criminal liability is an allegation by prosecutors, legislators or media; presumption of innocence applies to every person named. No court has ruled on guilt.
- Pages were read through an automated fetch/summary tool; quotes are as returned by that tool and should be re-checked against the original page before being printed verbatim.
- Known discrepancies between outlets are flagged inline (date of BCP resolution: 2 vs 3 Nov; date Peña sold his stake: "late 2024/early 2025", March 2025 or April 2025; size of public funds in Ueno: USD 800-850m range; number of detained executives).
- Outlet framing: ABC Color used loaded labels well before the collapse ("banco amigo de Santi Peña", "exsocios de Peña") - these are ABC's characterisations, not findings. La Nación (py) and Hoy coverage found is drier/judicial in tone and did not carry the procurement-link stories. International wires (AFP, EFE via regional papers, BBC Mundo) largely follow prosecutors' and Congress's documents.

## 1. Santiago Peña: ties to Grupo Vázquez/Ueno, loss of the presidency, charges, prosecutors, defence

### Takeaway
Peña offered his resignation on 5 Nov 2026, the Senate declined to process it and removed him by impeachment (44 votes, "mal desempeño") on 6 Nov; he was detained that night, placed in preventive detention on 7 Nov, charged with lesión de confianza (as instigator) and administración en provecho propio, and on 13 Nov additionally with asociación criminal as alleged "head of the structure". His defence (Ricardo Preda, Adolfo Ferreiro) says he never held shares in the bank itself, sold his Ueno Holding stake by early 2025 with Comptroller sign-off, did not direct any agency's deposits, and expects dismissal.

## 2. Central Bank officials: Carvallo, Insfrán, González, Caballero, Mora

### Takeaway
By 13 Nov 2026 the entire former BCP leadership named in the brief had been charged: all five with lesión de confianza and asociación criminal, Carvallo and González additionally with false-public-document offences. Prosecutors allege they knew of Ueno's insolvency and withheld or altered reports to the Senate; very little has been reported from them or their lawyers (Carvallo declined to testify and said he fears for his life).

## 3. Public funds in Ueno Bank (IPS and other state bodies), 2023-2026

### Takeaway
State and pension money in Ueno grew from one G. 70-80 billion IPS certificate in Sept 2023 to roughly USD 800-850 million by Sept 2026, about USD 355 million of it IPS, making Ueno the second-largest state depositary. The IPS Governing Council under Jorge Brítez loosened its placement rules in Nov 2023 and Nov 2024; critics allege steering, while Peña's defence says each body decided on its own analysis.

### Cited Findings
- Sept 2023: IPS CDA of G. 70,000 million at Financiera Ueno, a month after Peña took office; Dec 2025: G. 3.2 trillion + USD 144.6m in state deposits ("4,600% in 28 months", guaraní only); Ueno ranked second behind state-owned BNF by Dec 2025 — [Prensa Mercosur reproducing ABC Color, 20 Mar 2026](https://prensamercosur.org/2026/03/20/fondos-publicos-en-ueno-se-dispararon-4600-en-28-meses-de-gestion-de-pena/)
- End-Oct 2025: USD 734,085,541 of public funds per MEF Treasury report; IPS USD 333.9m (Sept 2025, 45.49%); +970% Jan 2024-Oct 2025 — [ABC Color, 15 Dec 2025](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2025/12/15/banco-amigo-de-santi-pena-recibio-us-730-millones-del-estado-e-ips/)
