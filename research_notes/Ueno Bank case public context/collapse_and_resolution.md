# Collapse and regulatory resolution of Ueno Bank S.A. (Paraguay) — public reporting to 20 November 2026

Researcher notes on method and limits (read first):
- All events from mid-2026 onward post-date my training data; everything below comes from pages retrieved on 20 Nov 2026. Each bullet gives outlet + publication date.
- The BCP's own press release page (bcp.gov.py) and MarketData's reproduction of it could NOT be fetched (site errors). The BCP communiqué is therefore recorded only as relayed by La Nación, ABC Color, Revista Foco, Forbes Argentina and AP.
- "Resolution No. 1 of 2 November 2026" (the number given in the assignment) was NOT confirmed by any source I could open: La Nación, ABC and 5Días all give the date and unanimity but no resolution number.
- Guaraní notation: Paraguayan "billón" = 10^12. "G. 1,62 billones" = 1.62 trillion guaraníes (short scale). USD conversions vary by outlet; I report them as printed.
- Everything about wrongdoing is allegation; the asserting party is named in each bullet. Named individuals appear only in their publicly reported institutional roles.

## 1. Corporate history: Grupo Vázquez, Financiera El Comercio, Ueno brand, licence, Visión Banco, size

### Takeaway
Ueno grew from a finance company bought by Grupo Vázquez in 2021 into a licensed bank (BCP approval 22 Nov 2023, operations 1 Dec 2023) and absorbed Visión Banco in June 2024; by 2026 it claimed about 3 million customers (roughly 60% of adults), about US$2.8bn of deposits and 8.9% of system deposits. Only about 1.82 million accounts turned out to hold a balance.

### Cited Findings
- Group origin: Credicentro founded 1984; Grupo Vázquez acquires Financiera El Comercio in 2021; Credicentro renamed ueno Holding in 2022 — [Forbes Argentina, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/de-financiera-banco-tres-millones-clientes-ueno-entidad-intervenida-paraguay-n101669)
- BCP approved conversion of Financiera Ueno into a bank on 22 Nov 2023 (per the bank's audited statements); banking operations began 1 Dec 2023 — [Forbes Argentina, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/de-financiera-banco-tres-millones-clientes-ueno-entidad-intervenida-paraguay-n101669); same 22 Nov 2023 date in [5Días special "Ueno: anatomía de una crisis", coverage to 18 Nov 2026](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)

- Late warning signs: Fitch kept BB but moved outlook to negative in Oct 2026 (rapid balance-sheet growth, intangibles); on 29 Oct 2026 Ueno announced a US$100m capital plan (US$50m from U Holdings, US$50m share sale to local private investors), subject to approvals — [Forbes Argentina, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.forbesargentina.com/negocios/de-financiera-banco-tres-millones-clientes-ueno-entidad-intervenida-paraguay-n101669)

### Inferences
- The 3 million "customers" versus 1.82 million funded accounts gap means customer-count claims overstated the economically active base by roughly 40%.
- US$2.8bn deposits and 8.9% system share are mutually consistent with a system of roughly US$30bn in deposits (not sourced directly).

### Gaps
- No source opened gives total assets at a recent date, the Visión Banco purchase price, or the date/price of the Financiera El Comercio acquisition beyond "2021".
- The date the "ueno" brand was first applied to the finance company was not found (only: Credicentro renamed ueno Holding in 2022).
- Ueno's own audited 2025 statements exist at ueno.com.py (found in search, not opened).

## 2. What the BCP and the Superintendency of Banks found, and when

### Takeaway
The BCP board voted unanimously on 2 Nov 2026 to open a resolution under Law 2.334/03 because, after adjustments made during a regularisation process, solvency fell below 50% of the minimum (5Días: reported 13.85%, minimum 12%, adjusted 5.56%). The supervisory trail visible in the press runs from an Aug 2025 SIB portfolio report, through a 5 Jun 2026 related-party excess notice and a 19/29 Jun 2026 board minute and memo, to a public denial of any regularisation plan on 14 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Decision: BCP Directorio resolved unanimously on 2 Nov 2026; communiqué issued in the early hours of 3 Nov; press conference at 07:00 on 3 Nov — [La Nación (py), 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/el-bcp-dispone-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/); [ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-bcp-anuncia-procedimiento-de-resolucion/)
- Legal basis: Law No. 2.334/03 "De garantía de depósitos y resolución de entidades de intermediación financiera"; no specific articles cited in press — [ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-bcp-anuncia-procedimiento-de-resolucion/); [La Nación, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/el-bcp-dispone-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/)
- Stated cause: solvency ratio below 50% of the required minimum, attributed by the BCP to "ajustes realizados a sus estados financieros durante el proceso de regularización" — [La Nación, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/03/el-bcp-dispone-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/)
- Ratios: reported 13.85%; regulatory minimum 12%; after adjustments 5.56% — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)
- Provisions deficit: G. 1.62 trillion ("1,62 billones"), per a report dated 4 Nov 2026 — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno). The BCP's civil embargo request the day before cited G. 1.6 trillion "for the deficit" — [Última Hora, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.ultimahora.com/embargan-por-usd-70-millones-los-bienes-de-directivos-de-ueno-bank-y-del-grupo-vazquez)
- BCP characterisation on 3 Nov: "indicios" of loans to companies linked to the controlling group, weaknesses in credit operations, deteriorating liquidity; described as the result of several months of technical work; situation specific to Ueno, not systemic — [Revista Foco, 3 Nov 2026](https://revistafoco.com.py/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-alcance-de-la-resolucion-y-los-proximos-pasos-del-bcp/)
- Interveners: the Superintendencia de Bancos was to designate interveners to verify the bank's net-worth position at the resolution date; none named — [ABC Color, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/11/03/ueno-bank-el-bcp-anuncia-procedimiento-de-resolucion/). A BCP "Equipo Interventor" was issuing notices by 12 Nov (headline) — [BCP, c. 12 Nov 2026](https://www.bcp.gov.py/web/institucional/w/el-equipo-interventor-del-bcp-informa-sobre-la-exoneracion-de-intereses-moratorios-y-punitorios-para-deudas-en-ueno-bank-sa)
- Supervisory chronology before resolution:
  - 29 Aug 2025: SIB report on loan classification found no adequate accounting separation to identify the past-due portfolio covered by the merger facility — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)
  - Dec 2025: prosecutors later allege BCP directors had "conocimiento directo y temprano" of insolvency from at least this date (allegation by Ministerio Público) — [ABC Color, 14 Nov 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/policiales/2026/11/14/ueno-bank-ordenan-inhibir-bienes-y-embargar-cuentas-de-exdirectivos-del-bcp/)
  - 5 Jun 2026: SIB notified an excess in related-party exposure; per the BCP, Ueno corrected it on 15 Jun — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno). AP: central bank "warned Ueno in June about excessive lending to companies tied to its owners" — [AP via KSAT, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/11/03/paraguay-seizes-major-digital-bank-tied-to-president-leaving-millions-of-customers-in-limbo/)
  - June 2026: after the SIB required divestment of related loans, Ueno transferred a US$74.4m loan portfolio (exposure to Grupo Hendy, Grupo M, MOWI) to related company Vinanzas SA; BCP found signs Vinanzas was indirectly financed by the bank itself (Última Hora as cited by Infobae; prosecutors argue this could have concealed the true position) — [Infobae, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/11/06/una-por-una-las-claves-para-entender-el-escandalo-del-ueno-bank-en-paraguay-que-derivo-en-una-fuerte-crisis-politica/)
  - 19 Jun 2026: BCP board Acta No. 27 records observations; 29 Jun 2026: memo signed by BCP director Carmen Marín sent to the SIB (then headed by Holger Insfrán) — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/); [ABC Color, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/19/filizzola-exige-al-bcp-detalles-sobre-la-fusion-de-ueno-bank-y-advierte-sobre-riesgos-para-los-depositos/)
  - Sept 2026: Senate information request with 31 questions; Contraloría audit on Ueno's rising share of IPS guaraní CDs — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)
  - 14 Oct 2026: Ueno denied being intervened or under a regularisation plan; BCP said excesses were corrected on time — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)
  - 29 Oct 2026: US$100m capitalisation announced; BCP director Carmen Marín resigned the same day — [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)
- BCP criminal complaint (as summarised by Hoy; allegations by the BCP/SIB): disproportionate loans to group companies; signature-only loans up to ten years without collateral; files lacking audited balance sheets; overdrafts covered with 3-day cheques or new loans; funds moved on quickly to ITTI S.A.E.C.A., REDISA and REDIPRO; a "simulated" sale of US$74.4m of overdue debt to Vinanzas S.A. via same-day loans to Satec S.A. and HD Invest E.A.S.; concealment of a single risk unit — [Hoy, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/04/timba-con-dinero-publico-denuncia-del-bcp-revela-perverso-esquema-de-ueno)
- Prosecutors (Arce, Corbeta, Adle, Cabrera) allege that in 2025–2026 loans and overdrafts went to same-group companies outside normal credit criteria; 27 people named in the request (8 directors/syndics, 19 linked to borrower companies) — [Infobae, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/11/06/una-por-una-las-claves-para-entender-el-escandalo-del-ueno-bank-en-paraguay-que-derivo-en-una-fuerte-crisis-politica/); [5Días special](https://5dias.com.py/especiales/ueno)

### Inferences
- The public record contains an apparent contradiction the report writer should flag: on 14 Oct 2026 the bank denied any regularisation plan, while the BCP's 3 Nov communiqué says the fatal adjustments were made "during the regularisation process". Either a regularisation process began between 14 Oct and 2 Nov, or the earlier denial was inaccurate; no source opened resolves this.
- A fall from 13.85% to 5.56% on a G. 1.62tn provisions deficit implies risk-weighted assets on the order of G. 19–20tn; this is my arithmetic, not a reported figure.

### Gaps
- Resolution number ("Resolución N° 1") not confirmed in any opened source; BCP site unreachable.
- No dates for on-site inspections, nor the start date or terms of the regularisation plan, in any opened source.
- The itemised adjustments (which assets were written down) were not found beyond the aggregate provisions deficit.
- Names of the interveners not found.

## 4. Since resolution: asset/liability transfer, payouts, embargoes, interveners

### Takeaway
An initial G. 10m advance was replaced by a Law 7745 advance of up to 40 minimum wages (G. 121.76m), paid from 5 Nov; by 9–12 Nov over 93–97% of depositors had been paid. As of 19 Nov no assets or deposits had been transferred: the BCP was still assembling lots for a competitive tender and the exclusion balance had been postponed 15 days on 18 Nov.

### Cited Findings

## 5. The 2024 "feasibility plan" for the Visión merger and regulatory forbearance

### Takeaway
ABC Color (16 Oct 2026), citing an internal BCP memo, reports that the BCP approved a feasibility plan on 21 Jun 2024 with two facilities worth G. 5.1tn (about US$691m) that let Ueno defer merger costs and park Visión's past-due loans; the memo flagged US$20m of "non-enabled" expenses and payments to group companies. Forbearance is alleged by prosecutors, an opposition senator and a banking executive; it is not an established finding.

### Cited Findings
- 27 May 2024: BCP board authorised two facilities totalling G. 5.1tn (about US$691m) for the merger; 21 Jun 2024: feasibility plan approved — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)
- Facility 1: expenses-and-investments trust, G. 2.5tn (about US$335m), for merger legal, consulting, systems and restructuring costs, booked as deferred charges authorised by the BCP. Facility 2: past-due loan facility, G. 2.6tn (about US$356m), covering Visión's overdue loans and loans up to 180 days overdue at merger, not to accrue interest — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)
- 13 Mar 2025: US$20m addendum (G. 155,698m) to the expenses trust; the memo says this fell outside permitted expenses ("gastos no habilitados") and needed prior board approval that apparently was not obtained — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)
- Memo findings (source: 29 Jun 2026 memo by a BCP director, based on 19 Jun 2026 board minutes; ABC did not independently verify): sample of G. 1.6tn (66% of trust) reviewed, support received for G. 1.5tn; some invoices predate merger approval; suppliers included Grupo Vázquez companies (Itti Saeca, Ueno Holding); recommends restrictive reading and exhaustive control — [ABC Color, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/16/ueno-advertian-sobre-gastos-no-habilitados-por-us-20-millones/)
- Senator Filizzola's 19 Oct 2026 request asked the BCP to recompute solvency if deferred merger expenses, losses and provisions were recognised immediately and equity stakes fully deducted, and whether prudential breaches already met Law 2334/2003 grounds; he alleged the BCP "ocultó" information from Congress (his assertion; no BCP reply in article) — [ABC Color, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/10/19/filizzola-exige-al-bcp-detalles-sobre-la-fusion-de-ueno-bank-y-advierte-sobre-riesgos-para-los-depositos/)

### Inferences
- The two facilities (G. 5.1tn) are about three times the G. 1.62tn provisions deficit later identified; if deferred items had been recognised up front, reported solvency would plausibly have breached the minimum well before November 2026. This is the mechanism Filizzola's questions point to, but no source quantifies it.
- The G. 5.1tn in facilities equalled roughly a quarter of the bank's US$2.8bn deposit base, which indicates the scale of regulatory accommodation relative to the institution.

### Gaps
- The text of the feasibility plan and of the 27 May 2024 board resolution is not public in any source opened; amortisation schedule unknown.
- No BCP or Ueno response to the memo's findings was reported in the ABC piece.
- Which "norms were changed" (Ayala) is unspecified; IPS investment-rule changes are suggested by ABC headlines from July 2026 (not opened).
