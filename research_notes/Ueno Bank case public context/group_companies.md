## 1. For each company: line of business, publicly reported shareholders and officers, relation to Grupo Vázquez, and reported loans or transfers

### Takeaway
Regulators (BCP/SIB) and prosecutors describe a single "unit of decision and control" in which Ueno Bank lent to formally separate companies whose funds were routed at once to ITTI, Redisa, Redipro, Vinanzas and Grupo Vázquez; the 15 June 2026 sale of a USD 74.4 million impaired portfolio to Vinanzas was, per the SIB, funded by the bank's own loans to SATEC, HD Invest, Technoma, Factopar and La Tropa Fletes. Ownership data is good for Grupo Vázquez, Mowi, and the charged officers of six borrowers; it is thin or absent for SATEC, HD Invest, Factopar, Office Design, Grupo Berilo, Henrici, Kaitel, and nothing was found on Data Force, COMITIA or Mulair.
