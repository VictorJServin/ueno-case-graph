## 1. Who is charged (27 imputados) and in what reported role

### Takeaway
The Fiscalía charged 27 people on 4 Nov 2026 in Causa 89/2026 "Federico Miguel Vázquez Villasanti y otros s/ Lesión de Confianza y otros": eight from the bank's board/oversight and 19 linked to borrower or group companies (Mowi, Grupo Hendy, Grupo M, ITTI, TUIU, Franede/Frandede, AIME). A 28th person (a Ueno Casa de Bolsa employee) was later detained on a separate obstruction-type charge. Outlets disagree on some job titles.

## 2. Offences, prosecutors and judges

### Takeaway
The original charges (4 Nov) were four: lesión de confianza, conducta indebida en situaciones de crisis, tentativa de estafa and violación del deber de llevar libros de comercio. On Tuesday 10 Nov the specialised Appeals Tribunal, of its own motion, added lavado de activos and asociación criminal and recast the fraud count as estafa agravada (consummated); defences have challenged that. Prosecutors Yeimy Adle, Luz Guerrero and Luis Piñánez are all on the team; the case judge is Rodrigo Estigarribia.

## 3. Arrests, detention, hearings, seizures and embargoes (chronology)

### Takeaway
Arrest orders for the eight were issued on 3 Nov; six were sent to pre-trial prison and two to house arrest on 5 Nov, although the Fiscalía had asked for prison only for Vázquez. The Appeals Tribunal upheld detention on 10 Nov. Astigarraga surrendered on 12 Nov and is also in pre-trial prison. Large asset seizures and a civil embargo sought by the BCP followed.

## 4. Julio Rey's statement to the Fiscalía

### Takeaway
The press reports a voluntary statement by Julio Rey in mid-November (18 Nov per El Nacional and RDN; "last Tuesday" per Extra), not one on 4 Nov. Coverage is second-hand, through his lawyer Guillermo Ferreiro, and mentions an internal document called "Delirio Colectivo". ## 6. How the prosecution describes the scheme: Vinanzas portfolio sale and related-party loans

### Takeaway
The prosecution's theory, as reported, is that between 2025 and 2026 the bank's leadership authorised loans and overdrafts to group-linked companies without adequate guarantees, moved the money among group entities, and on 15 June 2026 "sold" a USD 74.4 million portfolio of those loans to Vinanzas S.A. in a transaction allegedly financed indirectly with the bank's own money, masking its true financial position. Company-by-company loan amounts are not public in the pieces read, and Pagopar is not named.

### Cited Findings
- Core allegation: directors "habrían autorizado y permitido operaciones financieras entre 2025 y 2026" that harmed the bank's assets, which were mainly third-party funds the bank had to return (La Nación, 4 Nov 2026) — [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.py/judiciales/2026/11/04/caso-ueno-bank-imputan-a-27-personas-y-piden-prision-de-miguel-vazquez/)
- Per the imputación as reported by Independiente: loans granted 2025–2026 to linked companies without sufficient real guarantees and in disproportionate amounts; in Oct 2025 the bank asked for Mowi S.A., Grupo Hendy S.A. and Grupo M S.A. to be declassified as related parties without filing the required documents, yet kept granting loans and overdrafts; funds were transferred to ITTI S.A.E.C.A., Redisa, Redipro, Vinanzas and Grupo Vázquez — [Independiente](https://independiente.com.py/caso-ueno-bank-fiscalia-imputa-a-miguel-vazquez-y-otras-26-personas-por-presunto-esquema-de-operaciones-financieras-irregulares/)
